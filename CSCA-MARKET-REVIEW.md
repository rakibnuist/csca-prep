## 2. What the data says

1. **Depth wins single queries.** The "CSCA Test Guide" result is a 7,751-word page. Our `/csca` is
   1,949 words. Nothing beats a genuinely complete answer.
2. **Dates and calendar intents are cheap.** A 160-word page ranks for "CSCA exam dates 2026".
   We have a better calendar and lose because the page is thin and not titled for the query.
3. **Year-stamped titles dominate** (`2026`, `2026–2027`) in 8 of 10 SERPs. Titles are still
   `… · CSCA Master` without the year on half our pages.
4. **FAQPage schema is table stakes** among the ranking prep sites (csca.ai, csca.help, crossline).
   We have it on some pages, missing on dates/syllabus hub.
5. **Chinese-language demand exists** (cucas.cn, e-square, supercsca, sicas). We have zero zh pages.
6. **Nobody owns video** and nobody owns the "official client" story —
   our test-client simulation is a genuine differentiator with no search competition yet.
7. **Government and university domains** rank for "guide" and "instructions" intents. We cannot beat
   them on authority, so we win adjacent intents: mock tests, practice, score, preparation, dates.
8. **PDFs outrank HTML** for sample questions (scribd, china-admissions). A good HTML page with
   real, worked sample questions should beat a scanned PDF.
9. **Score intent is under-served**: passing score, validity, "what score do universities want",
   "can I retake" — answered by forum threads, not by a product page.
10. **Backlinks decide the head terms.** Nobody outranks csca.cn for "CSCA"; the win condition is
    owning every *modifier* query and the brand-adjacent long tail, then earning links from
    universities, agencies and forums.

## 4. Positioning that follows from the review

- **Head term:** we will not beat `csca.cn` for "CSCA". Target position 2–3 on the brand term and
  position 1 on every modifier: mock test, practice, past papers, syllabus, exam dates, passing
  score, test day, preparation.
- **Differentiators to lead every page with:** 176 papers (4× the nearest), free official archive,
  authentic 80/90 Chinese papers, and the only faithful test-client simulation.
- **Content moat:** one genuinely complete guide per intent, 2,000+ words, FAQPage schema, dated,
  sourced, plus 20–30 long-tail pages nobody bothers to write.
- **Distribution:** universities' international offices, scholarship agencies, Facebook groups,
  Reddit/Quora answers, and a YouTube explainer series — the channels where the questions already are.
