## 1. What the CSCA is (authentic)

| Fact | Value | Source |

| Full name | 来华留学本科入学学业水平测试 / China Scholastic Competency Assessment | csca.cn |
| Organiser | China Scholarship Council (CSC), 国家留学基金管理委员会, with Chinese university experts | csca.cn/about/examintro |
| Recognition | 300+ universities | csca.cn/about/examintro |
| Purpose | Standardised academic baseline for international undergraduate applicants; reference for admission and scholarship review. Not the sole criterion | csca.cn; CSCA Test Rules |
| Eligibility | Non-Chinese citizens applying for undergraduate study in mainland China. Chinese passports and HK/Macao/Taiwan residence permits are **not** eligible. No age limit; under-14 needs guardian consent | CSCA Test Rules / FAQ |

### Subjects and papers

| Paper | Questions | Time | Language | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professional Chinese — Humanities (文科中文) | 80 | 90 min | Chinese | 0–100 |
| Professional Chinese — STEM (理科中文) | 80 | 90 min | Chinese | 0–100 |
| Mathematics (compulsory) | 48 | 60 min | Chinese / English | 0–100 |
| Physics (elective) | 48 | 60 min | Chinese / English | 0–100 |
| Chemistry (elective) | 48 | 60 min | Chinese / English | 0–100 |

- Compulsory: Mathematics + Professional Chinese (CGS applicants, and Chinese-taught programmes).
  Physics/Chemistry are electives in the fee-and-subject structure; the exact combination is set by
  each receiving university.
- No negative marking; no national pass mark; universities set their own minimums.

### Professional Chinese item types (official, 2025 syllabus)

1. 识解汉字 — character recognition
2. 选词填空 — word-filling
3. 辨析词语 — word discrimination
4. 选词成段 — paragraph completion
5. 补全语句 — sentence completion
6. 阅读理解 — reading comprehension

Humanities topics: literature, philosophy, law, education, history, arts.
STEM topics: mathematical symbols and formulas, physical laws, chemical equations, lab
terminology, university-level maths/physics/chemistry basics, programming terms, data and graph
interpretation, academic reading.

### Content scope (condensed from the official syllabi)

- **Mathematics** — sets and inequalities; functions (domain/range, monotonicity, parity, power/
  exponential/log/trig, sequences, derivative basics); geometry and algebra (lines, circles,
  ellipses, hyperbolas, parabolas, vectors, complex numbers, spatial coordinate geometry);
  probability and statistics (classical probability, mean/variance, normal distribution basics).
- **Physics** — mechanics (kinematics, Newton's laws, momentum and impulse, work-energy, circular
  motion and gravitation, SHM and waves); electromagnetism (Coulomb, field strength and potential,
  DC circuits, magnetic induction, Ampere and Lorentz force, Faraday and Lenz); thermodynamics
  (kinetic theory, ideal gas law, first law); optics (reflection/refraction, interference,
  diffraction); modern physics (photoelectric effect, atomic structure, nuclear basics).
- **Chemistry** — matter classification and state changes; notation and equations; concentration
  and pH; amount-of-substance calculations; ideal gas law; inorganic properties; basic organic
  compounds; redox; ionic reactions and identification; atomic structure and periodic law; bonding
  and intermolecular forces; rate and equilibrium; electrolyte solutions; lab safety, gas
  preparation, separation and purification, industrial processes (e.g. ammonia synthesis).

### Calendar and fees

- Six sittings a year: **January, March, April, June, November, December**, over two days.
- Day 1: Professional Chinese 14:00–15:30, Mathematics 17:00–18:00 (Beijing).
  Day 2: Physics 14:00–15:00, Chemistry 16:30–17:30 (Beijing).
- Fees: **CNY 450** for one subject, **CNY 700** for two or more. Visa or Mastercard.
- Results: **within 10 working days**, electronic report in the candidate account.

## 2. How the real test day works (official Test Rules / Device & Environment)

### Delivery

- **RHT — remote-proctored home-based test (居家网考)**: own Windows computer, live proctoring.
  Only available where no test centre serves the region.
- **CBT — computer-based test at a centre (集中机考)**: authorised centre, centre equipment.

### Equipment (RHT)

- Genuine 64-bit **Windows 10/11** (or 7 SP1+). **macOS not supported.**
- ≥1366×768, ≥4 GB RAM, ≥2 GB free on the client partition.
- Webcam ≥720p at the top of the screen (built-in or USB); speakers + microphone.
  **Wireless headphones prohibited.** No privacy/anti-glare filters.
- Secondary device (phone/tablet) on a stand at ~45° rear-side, running the proctoring app.
- Network: **≥20 Mbps symmetric**, ≥500 KB/s to the platform; wired recommended; 4G hotspot backup.

### Room and tools

- Independent, quiet, well-lit, enclosed room; standard desk and chair; nobody else may enter.
- Scratch tool only: small whiteboard + erasable marker, **or** a blank sheet inside a transparent
  file bag with an erasable marker. Paper, sticky notes, pens and pencils are prohibited.
  Show it blank before, erase on camera after.

### Sequence on test day

1. 30–60 min before: log in, system/device/network check, connect secondary device by QR.
2. 15–30 min before: ticket number + password login, facial recognition, ID verification, 360°
   room scan, present ID and blank scratch tool.
3. 5 min before: read and accept the **Integrity Commitment Form**.
4. Countdown → automatic full screen → timer starts. Late arrivals are not admitted.
5. During: answers save continuously; **answered question numbers turn green in the palette on the
   left**; review and revise until time expires.
6. End: **automatic submission at zero — early submission is not permitted.** Wait for the
   submission confirmation, then erase the scratch tool on camera.

### Penalties

| Tier | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Minor | Affected test's results cancelled |
| Serious | Results cancelled + 2-year registration ban |
| Severe | All previous results cancelled + permanent ban |
| Extremely severe | Referred to judicial authorities; can affect study-in-China eligibility and visas |

Equipment, environment, power or network failures on the candidate's side: **fees not refunded**.
CSCA-side failures: CSCA may add time, reschedule, arrange a make-up test or refund.

## 4. What "same experience" means here — fidelity map

| Real client behaviour | Our reproduction | Honest limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-test system check (client, camera, mic, network) | `/exam/[slug]` gate runs real browser checks: online state, viewport ≥1024, fullscreen API, microphone permission — reported honestly as pass/warn | Cannot check the Windows client, disk space, or a second device |
| Ticket number + password login | Locally generated `CSCA-2026-XXXXXX` ticket strip, hidden by default | Not a real credential |
| Facial recognition, ID verification, 360° scan | Not simulated; explained in the pledge step | Impossible in a browser |
| Secondary-device proctoring | Explained; 45° requirement stated | No second camera |
| Integrity Commitment Form | Full text of the official commitments, explicit checkbox before start | Not binding |
| Countdown → full screen | 3-2-1 countdown, `requestFullscreen()` on start | User can exit full screen; we warn instead of locking |
| Left palette, answered = green | Implemented exactly (green answered numbers, amber flags, ringed current) | — |
| Continuous autosave | `POST /api/attempts/:id/save` every ~900 ms, resumes after a reload | — |
| No early submission | Submit disabled in **Official mode** until 00:00; **Practice mode** keeps early exit for drills | Deliberate, labelled |
| Auto-submit at zero | Server-anchored end time; client submits at 0; server closes at deadline | — |
| Proctor watches focus | Blur event counts and shows a proctor-style warning; count passed to the result page | Not a real proctor |

## 5. Student value — what ships today

- 176 full-length papers: 5 originals, 150 practice sets, 15 official sittings, 6 review modules.
- 2,867 questions across 60 topics, bilingual where the official paper is bilingual.
- Paper lengths match the official format exactly (48/60 and 80/90), single-answer, 0–100 scale.
- Timer anchored server-side (a refresh cannot buy minutes), auto-submit, autosave, resume.
- Post-paper analytics: score, band, modelled percentile, per-topic mastery, weakest-first breakdown.
- Spaced review queue (1 → 3 → 6 → 12 → 21 days), study plan dated back from the sitting, daily quiz.
- Every question has a worked explanation; wrong answers feed the review queue.
- Free official-paper archive; Pro unlocks only extra attempts, never content.

## 7. Data-authenticity policy

1. Exam facts come from the CSCA portal, CSC syllabi, Test Rules and Device & Environment docs — dated
   and linked on the page that states them.
2. Transcribed official papers are attributed to the sitting; partial transcriptions say how many of
   the official questions they contain (e.g. "40 of the paper's 80").
3. Reconstructed papers are labelled Review; original papers are labelled original.
4. Percentiles, score bands and difficulty ratings are modelled and labelled as estimates — never
   presented as official cohort data.
5. Takedown: anyone holding rights to a paper can ask for removal at `hello@cscamaster.com`; we comply.
6. When an official source changes, the page is re-dated and `src/lib/test-day.ts`, `src/lib/site.ts`
   and `src/lib/exam.ts` are the single places to edit.
