Quadratic Formula

一元二次方程求根公式

Solves quadratic equations of the form ax² + bx + c = 0. Here, 'x' represents the roots, 'a', 'b', and 'c' are coefficients, and the term under the square root (b² - 4ac) is the discriminant.

$\[ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \]$
Proof

Union of Sets

集合的并集

The union of two sets A and B contains all elements that are in A, in B, or in both. Denoted by 'A ∪ B'.

$\[ A \cup B = \{ x \mid x \in A \text{ or } x \in B \} \]$
Proof

Intersection of Sets

集合的交集

The intersection of two sets A and B contains all elements that belong to both A and B. Denoted by 'A ∩ B'.

$\[ A \cap B = \{ x \mid x \in A \text{ and } x \in B \} \]$
Proof

Complement of a Set

集合的补集

The complement of a subset A with respect to a universal set U contains all elements in U that do not belong to A. Denoted by ∁_U A.

$\[ \complement_{U} A = \{ x \in U \mid x \notin A \} \]$
Proof

Rational Inequality Equivalence

分式不等式的等价转化

Solves fractional inequalities by converting the division into multiplication. It maintains sign properties while ensuring the denominator 'cx + d' is non-zero.

$\[ \frac{ax + b}{cx + d} \le 0 \iff (ax + b)(cx + d) \le 0 \quad \text{and} \quad cx + d \ne 0 \]$
Proof

AM-GM Inequality

基本均值不等式

The Arithmetic Mean-Geometric Mean inequality states that for any non-negative real numbers 'a' and 'b', their arithmetic mean is greater than or equal to their geometric mean. Equality holds if and only if a = b.

$\[ \frac{a + b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab} \quad (a, b \ge 0) \]$
Proof

Change of Base Formula

对数的换底公式

Allows logarithm evaluation in any desired base 'c' (usually base e or base 10) by expressing it as the ratio of two logarithms in that base.

$\[ \log_a b = \frac{\log_c b}{\log_c a} \quad (a, b, c > 0, \, a, c \ne 1) \]$
Proof

Logarithmic Properties

对数的基本运算性质

Core operational laws of logarithmic functions: the log of a product is the sum of the logs, and the log of a quotient is the difference of the logs.

$\[ \log_a (xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y \quad \text{and} \quad \log_a \left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_a x - \log_a y \]$
Proof

Trigonometric Pythagorean Identity

同角三角关系（平方关系）

The fundamental relation expressing the Pythagorean theorem in terms of trigonometric functions. Useful for finding sine if cosine is known, or vice versa.

$\[ \sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1 \]$
Proof

Double-Angle Formula (Sine)

正弦的二倍角公式

Expresses the sine of double an angle in terms of the sine and cosine of the single angle.

$\[ \sin 2\theta = 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta \]$
Proof

Double-Angle Formula (Cosine)

余弦的二倍角公式

Expresses the cosine of double an angle in terms of the single angle's cosine and sine.

$\[ \cos 2\theta = \cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta \]$
Proof

Function Parity (Odd & Even)

函数的奇偶性

An even function is symmetric about the y-axis, satisfying f(-x) = f(x). An odd function is symmetric about the origin, satisfying f(-x) = -f(x). Both require a domain symmetric about x = 0.

$\[ \text{Even: } f(-x) = f(x), \quad \text{Odd: } f(-x) = -f(x) \]$
Proof

椭圆的标准方程

The standard equation of an ellipse centered at the origin with foci on the x-axis. Satisfies the focal relation c² = a² - b², where 'c' is the focal coordinate and 'e = c/a' is the eccentricity.

古典概型概率计算公式

Calculates the probability of an event 'A' in a classical probability model. 'N(A)' is the number of outcomes favorable to event 'A', and 'N' is the total number of equally likely outcomes in the sample space.
