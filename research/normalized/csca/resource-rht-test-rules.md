# 考试规则

1. 考前测试与准备
• 考试系统个人测试：考生须在考前按通知完成考试系统与设备的个人测试，验证客户端、摄像头、麦克风及网络环境是否符合要求。
• 集中模拟测试：所有考生均须按要求至少参加并完成一场集中模拟测试，以便提前发现并解决技术或设备问题，熟悉考试流程，为顺利参加正式考试做好充分准备。
⚠ 未按要求参加或完成以上两项测试，导致正式考试当天无法正常参加或完成考试的，相关责任由考生本人承担，考试费用不予退还。
2. 登录与核验时限：
CSCA考试客户端于每场考试开考前60分钟开放登录，最晚登录时间为开考前30分钟。考生须按要求完成设备检测、人脸识别与身份验证等全部考前流程。考试开始后，迟到考生无法进入考试系统。
3. 身份验证
• 考生须凭准考证号与密码登录考试系统。
• 考生必须通过人脸识别及有效身份证件原件的在线核验，且证件信息须与准考证信息完全一致。
4. 监控要求
• 全程开启：主、副机位摄像头、麦克风须全程开启，不得以任何形式遮挡或关闭。
• 画面质量：画面须清晰、光线充足，能清楚辨识考生面部特征与动作。
• 拍摄角度：主机位应拍摄考生正面上半身。副机位应置于考生侧后方45°角，完整覆盖考生侧脸、肩膀、手臂、双手及主机位屏幕。
• 声音监控：麦克风须正常收音，用于采集环境音。
• 设备状态：副机位设备须保持稳定供电，退出所有非必要应用， 并开启“勿扰模式”，同时使用“飞行模式 + Wi-Fi”设置（先开启飞行模式，再手动连接 Wi-Fi），以杜绝干扰。
5. 考试纪律
• 设备与软件：禁止切屏、多屏显示、运行非考试程序、远程控制及屏幕共享；严禁使用手机、计算器及其他未经允许的电子设备；严禁使用任何类型的无线耳机。
• 行为规范：考试全程不得离席、与他人交流，不得佩戴饰物遮挡面部；必须始终保持在监考画面内。
• 诚信与保密：严禁任何形式的作弊行为。不得以任何形式泄露、记录、传播试题与答案；不得寻求或提供任何外部帮助。
• 交卷规则：考试时间结束时系统将自动收卷，考生不得提前交卷。
重要提示：考生须服从监考员的一切指令。完整规定请参阅《考生须知与声明》与《诚信考试承诺书》。
6. 违规处理
违规行为一经查实，将根据情节轻重和影响后果，采取以下一种或多种处理：
• 取消当科考试成绩；
• 取消当次全部考试成绩；
• 自违规之日起两年内不得再次报考；
• 取消所有历史成绩；
• 将违规情况通知已接收该生CSCA成绩的国内院校；
• 终身禁止参加 CSCA 考试；
• 将移交司法机关依法处理。

## English
1. Pre-Test Checks and Preparation
• Equipment and Software Check: Test takers must complete the Equipment and Software Check before the test as instructed. This check verifies that the test software, camera, microphone, and network environment meet the test requirements.
• Home-based Proctored Mock Tests: All test takers must participate in and complete at least one Proctored Mock Test as instructed. The mock test helps identify and resolve potential technical or equipment issues in advance, familiarize test takers with the test procedures, and ensure they are fully prepared for the official test.
⚠ Failure to participate in or complete either of the required pre-test checks may result in being unable to participate in or complete the official test on test day. Any resulting consequences shall be borne by the test taker, and the test fee will not be refunded.
2. Login and Verification Deadline
Login opens 60 minutes before each test. You must log in to the test software no later than 30 minutes before the test starts. Follow the instructions to complete all pre-test checks, including equipment checks, facial recognition and identity verification. Late arrivals will not be admitted and bear responsibility for missing the test.
3. Identity Verification
Candidates must log in using their Admission Ticket number and password, and complete facial recognition and ID verification. The ID information must match the details on the Admission Ticket exactly.
4. Monitoring Requirements
• Continuous Operation: Cameras and microphones on both the Primary and Secondary Devices must remain on throughout the entire test. They must not be covered, turned off, or obstructed in any way.
• Video Quality: The video feed must be clear and well-lit, allowing the proctor to clearly identify the candidate's facial features and movements.
• Camera Angle: The primary camera should capture the candidate's upper body from the front. The secondary device must be positioned at approximately a 45°rear-side angle behind the candidate, showing the side profile, shoulders, arms, hands, and the primary device screen clearly.
• Audio Monitoring: The microphone must function properly to capture ambient sound throughout the test session.
Device Status: The secondary device must be connected to a stable power supply. All non-essential applications must be closed, and “Do Not Disturb” mode must be activated. For optimal interference prevention, it is recommended to use the “Airplane Mode + Wi-Fi” configuration (enable Airplane Mode first, then manually reconnect to Wi-Fi).
5. Test Conduct
• Devices and Software: Switching screens, using multiple monitors, running non-test applications, remote access, or screen sharing are prohibited. Mobile phones, calculators, and any other unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited. The use of any type of wireless headset is strictly forbidden.
• Behavior: Candidates must not leave their seat, communicate with others, or wear accessories that obscure their face. They must remain clearly visible to the proctor at all times
• Integrity and Confidentiality: Any form of cheating, recording, or sharing test questions or answers is strictly prohibited. Candidates must not seek or provide external assistance in any form.
• Submission: The system will automatically submit the test at the end of the allotted time. Early submission is not permitted.
⚠ Important Note: Candidates must follow all instructions given by the proctor. Please refer to the Test Guidelines and Declaration and the Integrity Commitment Form for complete regulations.
6. Consequences of Misconduct
If misconduct is confirmed, one or more of the following actions may be taken, depending on the nature and severity of the violation and its impact:
• Cancellation of the score for the affected test subject;
• Cancellation of all scores from the current test administration;
• A two-year ban from registering for any CSCA test, starting from the date of the violation;
• Cancellation of all previous CSCA scores;
• Notification of the misconduct to Chinese institutions that have received the test taker’s CSCA scores;
• A permanent ban from taking the CSCA test;
• Referral of the case to law enforcement authorities, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
