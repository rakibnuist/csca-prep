# 报名须知

1. 报名方式
考生须通过 CSCA 官方报名网站（www.csca.cn），使用个人长期可用的邮箱注册账户并完成报名缴费。
请注意：一个账户（邮箱）仅可绑定一名考生信息，请使用本人信息完成报名。请勿将个人账户借给他人使用或与他人共享，由此产生的后果由考生本人承担。
2. 报名截止
请登录报名系统或查阅官网公告，了解当期考试的具体报名截止时间。
3. 信息准确性
报名时，请务必确保所填写的个人信息（姓名、证件号码等）真实、准确，并与考试当天出示的有效证件信息完全一致，否则将无法参加考试。
4. 费用支付
考试费用请在报名时通过线上支付。具体费用标准及支持的支付方式（如VISA、MasterCard、微信、支付宝等）请参见报名页面的实时公示。
5. 准考证
准考证将在考前规定时间内开放下载，下载通知会发送至您注册的邮箱。请及时登录官网下载并仔细核对， 确保准考证信息与考试当日所持有效证件完全一致，此为参加考试的必备凭证，信息不符者将无法参加考试。

## English
1.How to Register
Create an account at www.csca.cn using a long-term accessible personal email, complete the registration form, select your test, and pay the test fee.
Please note: Each account (email address) can be used to register only one candidate. Please use your own information to complete the registration. Do not lend your account to others or share it with anyone. Any consequences arising from such actions shall be borne by the candidate.
2. Registration Deadline
Refer to your account dashboard or official announcements for each session's deadline.
3. Information Accuracy
All personal data (name, ID number, etc.) is truthful, accurate, and exactly match the ID document presented on exam day; otherwise entry will be denied.
4. Fee Payment
Fees are paid online during registration. The applicable fees and accepted methods (e.g., Visa, MasterCard, WeChat, Alipay) are those displayed at the time of payment and are subject to change without prior notice.
5. CSCA Admission Ticket
Available for download during the designated window prior to the exam; you will receive an email notice. Log in to download and verify that all details match your ID. The Admission Ticket is mandatory for entry.
