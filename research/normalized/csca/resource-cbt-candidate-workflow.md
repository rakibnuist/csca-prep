# 考试日流程

1. 提前到达
• 请于考试开始前 40分钟 到达考点。
2. 入场检查与就座
• 出示并核验身份证件与准考证 → 系统签到（优先人脸识别，如有问题由监考员人工确认） → 按指引入座。
• 入座后：将身份证件和准考证放置在桌面左上角；检查考试机的屏幕、键盘、鼠标是否正常，如有异常请立即举手示意。
3. 登录系统
• 在考试机上输入准考证号及密码登录考试系统。
• 仔细阅读并确认《诚信考试承诺书》，等待开考。
4. 开考与作答
• 进入考试：倒计时结束后，系统自动进入全屏答题界面并开始计时。
• 答题与保存：使用鼠标直接作答，系统自动保存答题进度。请留意左侧题号颜色（绿色为已作答），并自行核查是否完成所有题目。
• 时间管理：可在考试时间内返回修改已答题目，请合理分配时间。
5. 异常处理
• 如果出现设备或系统异常（如死机、白屏、卡顿、掉线等），请立即举手示意，等待工作人员处理。
• 系统会自动保留已作答进度，问题解决后可继续完成考试，监考员将根据中断时间据实补时。
• 请勿自行重启电脑或擅自离场。
6. 结束与退场
• 考试时间结束时，系统会自动收卷。请保持界面静止，等待监考员确认。
• 监考员收回并清点草稿纸后，按指引有序退场，取回个人物品，离场时请保持安静，不要在考场内外聚集或喧哗。

## English
1. Arrive Early
* Arrive 40 minutes before your scheduled start time.
2. Check-in & Seating
* Present your ID and Admission Ticket → Complete system check-in (facial verification may be used; manual verification if needed→ Proceed to your assigned seat.
* After seating: Place your ID and Admission Ticket at the top-left of the desk; check if the computer screen, keyboard, and mouse are functioning normally. Raise your hand immediately to report any issue.
3. System Login
* On the exam computer, enter your test admission ticket number and password to log into the exam system.
* Read and agree to the Examination Integrity Pledge, then wait for the exam to begin.
4. Exam Start & Answering
* Start Exam: When the countdown ends, the test launches in full-screen and the timer starts.
* Answering & Saving: Use the mouse to answer on screen; progress is auto-saved. Monitor the question navigator on the left (e.g., green = answered) to track and ensure all questions are completed.
* Time Management: You may review and change answers within the allotted duration. Manage your time wisely.
5. Technical Issues
* If equipment or system malfunctions (e.g., freeze, blank screen, latency, disconnection), immediately raise your hand and wait for assistance.
* Answered progress is automatically saved. You may continue after resolution. Compensatory time may be granted to reflect the verified interruption.
* Do not restart the computer, troubleshoot on your own or leave your seat without permission.
6. Exam End & Dismissal
* When time expires, the system auto-submits your responses. Keep the screen as is and wait for proctor confirmation.
* Return all scratch paper(used and unused), do not remove any test materials from the room. Exit orderly and quietly as instructed. Do not congregate or make noise inside or outside the test area.
