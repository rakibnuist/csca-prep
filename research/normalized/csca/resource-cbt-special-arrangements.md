# 特殊考试安排

为保障特殊需求考生的考试公平性与顺利应试，考务管理机构可根据个案情况进行评估，并在条件允许的范围内提供合理的便利安排。
考生如需申请特殊考试安排，应至少于考试日前30天提交正式申请，并附上相关证明材料（如医院出具的医疗诊断、功能评估报告等）。
如因考点条件或考试形式所限，无法满足所申请的便利安排，我们将及时告知考生，并与其协商是否取消本次报名。如考生选择取消报名，将予以全额退费。

## English
To support candidates with documented special needs, the test administration will evaluate requests case-by-case and provide reasonable accommodations within operational feasibility.
* Submit your application at least 30 business days before your exam date, with supporting documents (e.g., an official medical diagnosis or functional assessment issued by a qualified hospital).
* If requested accommodations cannot be provided due to test center or delivery-mode limitations, you will be notified promptly and may cancel the registration for a full refund.
