# CSCA隐私保护指引

CSCA来华留学本科入学学业水平测试平台
个人信息与隐私保护指引

CSCA来华留学本科入学学业水平测试平台（以下简称“本平台”）高度重视考生的个人信息与隐私保护。本指引适用于所有报名参加CSCA考试的考生，涵盖集中机考、纸笔考试及居家网考三种考试形式。特此说明我们将如何依法收集、使用、存储与保护您的个人信息，并明确告知您在此过程中的知情权、选择权与控制权。
在开始注册并使用本平台前，请确认您具备中华人民共和国法律规定的与您行为相适应的民事行为能力。若您不具备前述与您行为相适应的民事行为能力，需要在法定监护人的监护参与下才能注册并使用本平台的服务，您及您的法定监护人应依照法律规定承担因此而导致的一切后果。

如果您不同意本指引的任何内容，请您停止注册。您停止注册后将无法享受本平台的服务。如您完成注册流程后，即表示您已充分阅读、理解并接受本指引的全部内容；同意我们可以依据本指引处理您的个人信息，同意我们将您的相关信息共享给为完成考试活动所必须的第三方。如您对本指引的内容有任何疑问，您可随时通过本指引中的联系方式联系我们。

1.信息收集类型与场景
注册阶段：在您注册并开始使用本平台前，我们会收集您的电子邮箱和密码，用于实现注册账号、登录平台、找回密码功能。如您不提供相应信息，您将无法使用我们的服务。
报名阶段：在您考试报名时，我们会收集您的考试日期、考试场次、考试地区、考试地点、考试城市、考试形式、考试科目、姓名、性别、出生日期、国籍、证件类型、证件号码、证件有效期、电子邮箱、电话、照片、常住地、就读院校、母语及其他与报考相关的信息。同时，我们将收集您法定监护人的姓名、手机号、电子邮箱以及该法定监护人与您的亲属关系信息。为了方便您报名，您也可将与报名有关的个人信息提供给我们的合作机构，并授权合作机构代您向本平台报名考试，将您的报名信息传输、共享给我们，用于完成您的报考流程。如您不提供相应信息，您将无法通过本平台报名及参与考试，但不影响您使用本平台其他功能。
考试阶段：
采用纸笔考试方式进行考试时，我们将收集您的姓名、身份证件信息、准考证号、试卷、答题卡、作答信息、照片、面部识别信息、考场监控（包括音、视频）。

采用集中机考方式进行考试时，我们将收集您在操作计算机进行考试过程中的所有信息，包括系统登录信息、作答信息、系统操作信息、身份核验信息、面部识别信息、照片、身份证件信息、考场监控（包括音、视频）。

采用居家网考方式进行考试时，我们将收集您的实时监控信息，包括身份证件信息、面部识别信息、房间环境信息、智能设备信息、智能设备桌面信息、作答信息、实施监控考试所用智能设备的摄像头、麦克风产生的音视频信息、考试过程中鼠标键盘轨迹、异常行为信息（如离屏、多人入场、异常声音或画面信息）。居家网考过程中，我们将通过本平台或相关软件调用您使用的智能设备的硬件功能，包括摄像头、麦克风、鼠标键盘轨迹记录等。

我们在考试阶段收集以上信息用于核验考生身份、成绩评定、成绩归属确认、考场管理、监考工作、识别作弊、成绩查询、报告生成。如您不提供相应信息，您将无法正常参与考试，但不影响您使用本平台其他功能。

考后服务阶段：我们将收集和处理与考试结果相关的信息，包括成绩评定、阅卷信息、考试成绩及成绩报告等，用于成绩评定、成绩查询、生成报告及后续服务。如您不提供相应信息，我们将无法对您的考试成绩进行评定，从而视为您没有考试成绩，亦无法向您提供成绩评定、成绩查询、生成报告等后续服务，但不影响您使用本平台其他功能。
支付信息：您可以选择通过第三方平台（包括支付宝、微信支付、VISA、MasterCard等支付渠道）缴纳CSCA考试费用。我们需要将您的订单号与交易金额信息与您选择的第三方平台共享以完成支付。在您使用支付功能时，第三方平台会收集、验证您的姓名、证件信息、银行卡卡号、有效期及其他必要信息，以完成支付，具体适用您选择的第三方平台的隐私政策。该等信息由第三方平台依法独立收集和处理。因我们不收集相关支付信息，故我们不对第三方平台收集、处理、使用您个人信息的行为及产生的任何后果承担法律责任。为了解您的支付进度及状态，我们会向您选择的第三方平台收集相应的支付状态信息，包括支付时间、支付方式、支付金额。如您不提供相应信息，您将无法正常使用支付功能，但不影响您使用本平台其他功能。
其他信息收集情形：若未来推出的新服务或功能涉及收集本指引未列明的个人信息，我们将相应更新本指引，并在收集前通过页面提示、功能说明、弹窗确认等方式，向您充分说明该信息的类型、用途与处理方式，征得您的明示同意后，方可进行相关信息的收集与使用。
在以上个人信息收集的类型与场景中，我们收集的个人敏感信息包括：身份证件信息（如您的姓名、出生日期、国籍、证件号码等）、生物识别信息（如您的面部识别信息、声音、照片、视频、书写样本）、电子邮箱及14周岁（含）以下儿童的个人信息。
2.信息的存储
CSCA考试过程中收集的个人信息在中华人民共和国境内进行存储和处理。
在确有需要的情况下（如成绩评定、证书生成或技术核查），相关信息将传输至中国境内，用于集中处理与服务交付。
无论您的个人信息被传输、存储或处理于何处，我们均将按照中华人民共和国法律、法规（不包括香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区及台湾地区的相关规定）及本指引的规定保护您的个人信息。
3.个人信息保存期限
注册账户信息：保存至用户申请注销。

试卷、作答信息：保存2年。

成绩信息：长期保存。

图像、音视频资料：长期保存。

6.您的权利
我们非常重视您对个人信息的关注，并尽全力保护您对于您个人信息访问、更正、删除以及撤回同意的权利，以使您拥有充分的能力保障您的隐私和安全。您的权利包括:

您可以通过发送电子邮件至 support@csca.cn 申请删除您的部分个人信息；如您希望删除全部个人信息，可申请注销账号。我们将在注销后根据适用法律要求，对您的个人信息予以删除或匿名化处理。
请您理解，本平台的各项功能均需要相应的个人信息才能得以实现。若您要求删除部分个人信息，我们将无法继续为您提供与该等信息相关的服务。届时，您将无法再使用该等服务（包括成绩查询、成绩报告等），您的历史成绩记录也可能无法继续保留。
您可以通过联系我们撤回部分或全部授权，或通过注销账号的方式撤回我们继续收集您个人信息的全部授权。
请您理解，本平台的每项功能均需要一些个人信息才能得以实现，当您撤回同意或授权后，我们无法继续为您提供撤回同意或授权所对应的服务，也不再处理您相应的个人信息。但您撤回同意或授权的决定，不会影响此前基于您的授权而开展的个人信息处理。
您可以通过发送电子邮件至 support@csca.cn 申请注销您的账号。
特别提示，您注销账号后，我们将被迫停止为您提供服务，并根据适用法律的要求删除您的个人信息，或进行匿名化处理。届时，您将无法使用本平台的任何服务,特别是成绩查询、成绩报告等，您的历史成绩记录也将无法继续保留。
7.Cookie与技术说明
为最大限度保护用户隐私，CSCA考试平台目前不使用Cookies、像素标签或其他自动化追踪技术。所有用户行为数据均为考试功能所需，不含广告追踪或第三方嵌入脚本。
8.未成年人信息保护
我们非常重视对未成年人个人信息的保护。若您是18周岁以下的未成年人，在使用本平台前，应事先取得您法定监护人的同意。
基本原则
法定监护人知情与监督：未满18周岁的考生需在法定监护人知悉并监督下完成账户注册、考试报名及相关政策确认。

信息最少够用：仅收集考试必需信息，且严格限制使用范围。涉及敏感信息时，仅限考试核验用途，不超范围采集或存储。

信息使用限制
匿名化处理：用于研究或统计时，所有数据均匿名化处理，确保无法识别个人身份。

禁用追踪技术：明确声明不对未成年人使用Cookie或其他自动化信息收集技术。

第三方协作
合作伙伴监管：若需与第三方共享信息（如考点、监考平台等），需法定监护人同意，并严格限制信息用途。

外部链接提示：本平台若含第三方链接（如支付平台），我们将予以标注，建议未成年用户在使用前与法定监护人共同审查其隐私政策。

权利与安全
账户管理：法定监护人可随时联系我们申请注销未成年人账户或删除数据。

通知权限：除考试必需通知（如准考证下载、考试时间安排等）外，CSCA官方推送活动信息、考试推广及教育类服务通知，需获得法定监护人授权。

法律例外
强制披露：仅在法律要求或保护未成年人安全时，未经同意可共享信息（如司法调查）。

9.更新与通知
为适应法律、技术及业务的发展，我们可能适时修订本指引。如有任何实质性变更，我们将通过官方网站公告、电子邮件或页面弹窗等方式，及时告知法定监护人及考生，以保障其知情权与选择权。
10.语言版本说明

## English
CSCA Personal Data & Privacy Protection Guidelines

The CSCA Testing Service Website (the “Website”) values the protection of test takers’ personal data and privacy. These Guidelines apply to all individuals who register for CSCA tests and cover the following delivery modes: CSCA Computer-based Test, CSCA Paper-based Test, and CSCA Home-based Test. This document explains how we lawfully collect, use, store, and protect your personal information and describes your rights to be informed, to choose, and to control your data.

Before you register for and use the Website, you must have the civil capacity appropriate to your actions as required under the laws of the People’s Republic of China (PRC). If you do not have the requisite civil capacity, you must register for and use the Website only with your legal guardian’s participation, and you and your legal guardian are required to bear all consequences arising therefrom in accordance with law.

If you do not agree to any part of these Guidelines, you must stop the registration process. If you stop registering, you will not be able to use the Website’s services. By completing registration, you acknowledge that you have fully read, understood, and accepted these Guidelines, consent to our processing of your personal information as described here, and agree that we may share your information with third parties as necessary to conduct the test. If you have any questions about these Guidelines, you may contact us at the email address provided below.
I. Types and Scenarios of Data Collection
Sign Up: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password to enable account creation, login, and password recovery. You will not be able to use our services if you do not provide this information.
Test Registration: When you register for a test, we collect your test date, test session, test region, test location, test city, test delivery mode, test subjects, name, gender, date of birth, nationality, ID type, ID number, ID expiration date, email address, phone number, photo, place of residence, current school, native language, and other registration-related information. We also collect your legal guardian’s name, mobile phone number, email address, and family relationship to you. For your convenience, you may provide registration information to our partners and authorize them to submit your information to the Website to complete your registration. If you do not provide the required information, you cannot register for or take the test via the Website. This does not affect your use of other Website features.
During Testing:
CSCA Paper-based Test: We collect your name, ID information, admission ticket number, test paper, answer sheet, responses, photo, facial recognition information, and test-site monitoring data (including audio and video).

CSCA Computer-based Test: We collect all information generated during your computer-based test session, including system login information, responses, system operation logs, identity verification information, facial recognition information, photo, ID information, and test-site monitoring data (including audio and video).

CSCA Home-based Test: We collect real-time monitoring data, including ID information, facial recognition information, room/environment information, smart-device information, smart-device desktop information, responses, audio and video captured by the camera(s) and microphone(s) of the device(s) used for proctoring, mouse and keyboard activity logs during the test, and anomalous behavior signals (for example, off-screen activity, additional person(s) entering the room, abnormal audio or video). During the CSCA Home-based Test, this platform or related software will access certain hardware features on your device, including the camera, microphone, mouse/keyboard activity logs and so on.

We use the above information to verify identity, evaluate and attribute scores, manage the test site/session, conduct proctoring, detect cheating, enable score inquiries, and generate reports. If you do not provide the required information, you will not be able to take the test. This does not affect your use of other Website features.
Post-Test Services: We collect and process score-related information (including scoring, marking/grading data, test scores, and score reports) for score evaluation, score inquiry, report generation, and follow-up services. If you do not provide the information required for these purposes, we cannot evaluate or report your scores, and you will be deemed not to have an official score. This does not affect your use of other Website features.
Payment Information: You may pay test fees through third-party payment channels (including Alipay, WeChat Pay, VISA, and MasterCard). We share your order number and transaction amount with the selected payment channel to complete the transaction. The payment channel will collect and verify your name, ID information, card number, expiration date, and other necessary information subject to its own privacy policy. Such information is collected and processed independently by the payment channel in accordance with law. Because we do not collect this payment information, we assume no legal liability for the third party’s collection, processing, or use of your personal information or any resulting consequences. To confirm your payment progress and status, we obtain payment status information (including payment time, method, and amount) from the selected payment channel. If you do not provide the necessary information, you cannot use the payment function. This does not affect your use of other Website features.
Other Scenarios: If new services or features in the future require collection of personal information not listed in these Guidelines, we will update these Guidelines and, before collection, provide clear notice (e.g., on-page prompts, feature descriptions, or pop-up confirmations) describing the type, purpose, and processing method of the additional information. We will collect and use such information only with your explicit consent.
Sensitive Personal Information: In the above scenarios, we collect certain sensitive data, including: ID information (e.g., your name, date of birth, nationality, ID number), biometric information (e.g., facial recognition data, voice, photos, videos, handwriting samples), your email address, and personal information of children aged 14 or under.
II. Data Storage
Personal information collected during CSCA testing is stored and processed within the territory of the People’s Republic of China.
Where necessary (e.g., for scoring, certificate generation, or technical verification), relevant information will be transmitted to China for centralized processing and service delivery.
Regardless of where your personal information is transmitted, stored, or processed, we protect it in accordance with PRC laws (excluding the laws and regulations of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan) and these Guidelines.
III. Data Retention Period
Account registration data: retained until the user requests account deletion.

Test papers and responses: retained for 2 years.

Score information: retained on a long-term basis.

Images and audio/video materials: retained on a long-term basis.

IV. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure
We take the protection of your personal information seriously and, as a rule, do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information. We may process or provide your information only in the following necessary circumstances:
With your explicit authorization and consent;

As necessary to perform testing services, sharing within the minimum scope required with entities providing test organization, test delivery, technical support, and other related services (including proctoring, test-site management, marking/grading, and score evaluation), as well as schools or other institutions receiving your scores;

As required by laws and regulations or pursuant to lawful instructions of competent authorities;

In emergencies to maintain international or public security or to protect the significant lawful rights and interests of others;

In corporate changes conducted in accordance with law (e.g., merger, division, dissolution, or bankruptcy). Where personal information is transferred due to such changes, we will notify you as required by law and continue to protect your information.

All third parties receiving information are required by us to maintain confidentiality, implement effective security measures, and must not use the information for unauthorized purposes.
V. Data Security Measures
We protect test taker information through industry-standard measures, including:
Technical measures: encryption of data in transmission and at rest, multi-factor authentication, access control, and security auditing;

Administrative measures: We operate a reliable, efficient incident-response mechanism, conduct continuous system monitoring, and maintain an up-to-date incident-response plan. If personal information is or may be leaked, altered, or lost, we will immediately take remedial measures and fulfill our notification and reporting obligations in accordance with law.

VI. Your Rights
We respect and protect your rights to access, correct, delete, and withdraw consent with respect to your personal information.
Access: You may log in to your CSCA registration account to view your data generated during registration, testing, and score management.

Correction: To correct personal information, follow the “Information Correction” process within the registration system. After you complete the process and your request is manually reviewed and approved, we will make timely corrections.

Deletion (may affect services): You may request the deletion of some personal information by sending an email to support@csca.cn. If you wish to delete all of your personal information, you may apply for account cancellation. After the cancellation is completed, we will delete or anonymize your personal information in compliance with applicable legal requirements.

Please understand that each function of the Website requires certain personal information to operate properly. If you request the deletion of part of your personal information, we will be unable to continue providing the services associated with that information. As a result, you will no longer be able to use such services (including score inquiry and report services), and your previous score records may no longer be retained.
Partial withdrawal of authorization (may affect services): You may withdraw some or all authorizations by contacting us, or withdraw all authorization for our continued collection of your personal information by canceling your account.

Please understand that each function of the Website requires certain personal information to operate properly. If you withdraw your consent or authorization, we will no longer be able to provide the services corresponding to the withdrawn consent or authorization, and we will stop processing the related personal information. However, your withdrawal of consent or authorization will not affect any personal information processing that has been carried out based on your prior consent.
Account cancellation: You may request cancellation by emailing support@csca.cn. Please note: After you cancel your account, we will stop providing all services to you and will delete or anonymize your personal information as required by applicable laws. You will no longer be able to use any services on the Website, including score inquiry and report functions, and your previous score records will no longer be available.

VII. Cookies and Technical Tracking
To maximize privacy protection, the CSCA testing website does not currently use cookies, pixel tags, or other automated tracking technologies. All user behavior data is limited to what is necessary to deliver testing functions; no advertising trackers or third-party embedded scripts are used.
VIII. Protection of Minors’ Personal Data
We attach great importance to the protection of minors’ personal information. If you are under 18 years old, you must obtain your legal guardian’s consent before using the Website.
Basic Principles
Legal Guardian awareness and supervision: Test takers under 18 must complete account registration, test registration, and related policy acknowledgments under the knowledge and supervision of a legal guardian.

Data minimization: We collect only information necessary for testing (e.g., name, date of birth, ID number) and strictly limit its use. Sensitive data is limited to identity verification and is not collected or retained beyond scope.

Use Restrictions
Anonymization: When used for research or statistical purposes, data is anonymized to prevent identification of individuals.

No tracking technologies: We do not deploy cookies or other automated tracking technologies for minors.

Third-Party Collaboration
Partner oversight: Where sharing with third parties (e.g., test sites, proctoring platforms) is necessary, legal guardian consent is required and the purpose of use is strictly limited.

External links: If the Website contains third-party links (e.g., payment platforms), we will indicate such links. Minors are advised to review those privacy policies together with their legal guardians before use.

Rights and Security
Account management: Legal guardians may contact us at any time to request account cancellation or data deletion for the minor.

Notifications: Except for test-critical notifications (e.g., Admission Ticket download, test time arrangements), CSCA activity updates, test promotion, and educational service notifications require legal guardian authorization.

Legal Exceptions
Compelled disclosure: Without consent, information may be shared only when required by law or to protect a minor’s safety (e.g., judicial investigations).

IX. Updates and Notifications
We may revise these Guidelines from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or business developments. In the event of any material changes, we will notify legal guardians and test takers via announcements on the official website, email, or in-page pop-ups to protect your right to be informed and to choose.
