5. 考试违规处理
5.1 本平台涵盖集中机考、纸笔考试及居家网考三种考试形式，用户在本平台报名考试，应当签署《CSCA考生诚信考试承诺书》并严格遵守。
5.2 考试违规行为包括以下行为：
5.2.1 考生不遵守考场纪律，不服从考试工作人员的安排与要求，有下列行为之一的，应当认定为考试违纪：
(1) 携带规定以外的物品进入考场或者未放在指定位置的；
(2) 未在规定的座位或机位参加考试，或擅自更换考试设备的；
(3) 考试开始信号发出前答题或者考试结束信号发出后继续答题的；
(4) 在考试过程中旁窥、交头接耳、互打暗号或者手势的；
(5) 在考试主办机构及其授权的考务执行机构禁止的范围内，喧哗、吸烟或者实施其他影响考场秩序的行为的
(6) 未经考试工作人员同意，在考试过程中擅自离开考场、机位或监控范围的；
(7) 将试卷、答卷（含答题卡、答题纸等，下同）、草稿纸等考试用纸带出考场的；
(8) 用规定以外的笔或者纸答题，或者在试卷规定以外的地方书写姓名、考号或者以其他方式在答卷上标记信息的；
(9) 拒绝或不配合身份核验、物品检查、考试环境检查、机位及设备调整、草稿工具检查等必要考试管理要求的；
(10) 在居家网考中，未经允许有除考生外的其他人员出现在考试房间内的，或考试环境不符合《CSCA考生须知与声明》或《CSCA居家网考设备与环境要求》相关规定的;
(11) 在居家网考中，未按要求配合将草稿材料进行展示、擦除或销毁的；
(12) 其他违反考试规则的行为。
5.2.2 考生违背考试公平、公正原则，在考试过程中有下列行为之一的，应当认定或视为考试作弊：
(1) 携带与考试内容相关的材料或者存储有与考试内容相关资料的电子设备参加考试的；
(2) 抄袭、协助他人抄袭，或者通过与他人交流、接受他人协助等方式获取或传递试题答案及其他与考试内容相关的信息的；
(3) 抢夺、窃取他人试卷、答卷或者胁迫他人为自己抄袭提供方便的；
(4) 携带或使用未经允许的设备、资料或其他物品的；
(5) 由他人代替参加考试的；
(6) 故意销毁试卷、答卷或者考试材料的；
(7) 在答卷上填写与本人身份不符的姓名、准考证号等信息的；
(8) 传、接物品或者交换试卷、答卷、草稿纸的；
(9) 拒不实时、同步提供考试音、视频数据资料，包括中断网络连接，遮挡、关闭摄像头，关闭麦克风，关闭监控页面或以其他方式影响正常监考的；
(10) 使用未经允许的硬件设备、软件、程序或其他技术手段的；
(11) 组织、参与考试作弊，或者为组织作弊提供人员、设备、账号、技术支持等帮助的；
(12) 其他以不正当手段实施考试作弊或协助他人作弊的行为。
5.2.3 考试主办机构及其授权的考务执行机构、考试工作人员在考试过程中或者在考试结束后发现下列情形之一的，应当认定相关的考生实施了考试作弊行为：
(1) 通过伪造证件、证明、档案及其他材料获得考试资格和考试成绩的；
(2) 评卷过程中被认定为答案雷同的；
(3) 考场纪律混乱、考试秩序失控，出现大面积考试作弊现象的；
(4) 考试工作人员协助实施作弊行为，事后查实的；
(5) 通过系统日志、数据分析、音视频监控记录或其他技术手段，经核查认定存在违规获取或试图获取试题、答案行为的；
(6) 其他应认定为作弊的行为。
5.2.4 考生及其他人员应当自觉维护考试工作秩序，服从考试工作人员的管理，不得有下列扰乱考试秩序的行为：
(1) 扰乱考点、考场、评卷场所等考试工作场所秩序；
(2) 拒绝、妨碍考试工作人员履行管理职责；
(3) 威胁、侮辱、诽谤、诬陷或者以其他方式侵害考试工作人员、其他考生合法权益的行为；
(4) 损坏考场设施设备；
(5) 其他扰乱考试管理秩序的行为。
5.2.5 考生不得有下列其他违规行为：
(1) 伪造、篡改、买卖、出租、出借或者冒用CSCA成绩、成绩报告、准考证或者相关证明材料的；
(2) 在考试结果已被撤销或认定无效后，仍向院校、机构或者其他第三方提交、使用原成绩报告的；
(3) 通过虚假材料、欺骗、胁迫、贿赂或者其他不正当手段要求修改考试数据、考试成绩或者违规处理决定的；
(4) 在违规调查、成绩核查或者复核过程中隐匿、转移、删除、篡改证据，或者指使他人提供虚假证据或协助掩饰、隐瞒违规行为的；
(5) 其他损害CSCA考试公平、公正、诚信、客观的不当行为。
5.3 考试违规行为的处理：
5.3.1 本平台有权依据本条规定及《CSCA诚信考试承诺书》对违反第5.2条及《CSCA诚信考试承诺书》的考生进行处理。
5.3.2 对考试过程中发现的异常、疑似违规或影响考试秩序的行为，我们及我们授权的考务执行机构有权根据实际情况采取提醒、警告、要求整改、暂停考试、终止考试等必要的考试管理措施。我们及我们授权的考务执行机构有权根据违规行为的性质、情节和影响作出相应处理，并有权将违规情况通知有关中国院校或其他机构；相关院校或机构有权依据其规定，采取取消录取资格或学籍、取消奖学金或其他相关资格等处理措施。
5.3.3 考生有第5.2.1、5.2.2、5.2.3、5.2.4、5.2.5条所列行为之一的，考试主办机构将依据事实和证据，根据情节轻重和影响后果，采取以下一种或多种处理：
(1) 取消考生当科考试成绩；
(2) 取消考生当次部分或全部科目考试成绩；
(3) 取消考生部分或全部历史考试成绩；
(4) 自违规之日起考生两年内不得再次报考；
(5) 将违规情况通知有关中国院校或其他机构；
(6) 终身禁止考生参加CSCA考试；
(7) 移交司法机关依法处理。

(1) bringing unauthorized items into the test room or failing to place them in designated areas;
(2) not testing at your assigned seat or workstation, or changing devices without authorization;
(3) beginning before the start signal or continuing after the end signal;
(4) peeking, whispering, or using hand signals/gestures during the test;
(5) making noise, smoking, or otherwise disturbing the test order in areas where it is prohibited by the organizer and its authorized providers;
(6) leaving the room, seat, or monitored area during the test without permission;

(10) allowing any other person to be present in the testing room without authorization during a home-based test, or failing to comply with the relevant provisions of the CSCA Test Taker Guidelines and Declaration or the CSCA Home-based Equipment & Environment Requirements regarding the testing environment;
(11) failing to show scratch paper or a whiteboard, erase all notes, or destroy scratch paper as instructed;
(12) any other violation of test rules.

(1) bringing materials related to the test content or devices storing such materials;
(2) copying or helping others copy answers or materials related to the test content, or obtaining or sharing test answers or other test-related information by communicating with others or accepting their assistance;
(3) seizing or stealing another person’s test materials, or coercing others to facilitate copying;
(4) bringing or using unauthorized devices, materials, or files;
(5) having another person take the test in your name;
(6) intentionally destroying test materials;
(7) writing a name or Admission Ticket number or other information that does not match your identity on an answer sheet;
(8) passing or receiving items, or exchanging test papers, answer sheets, or scratch paper;
(9) refusing to provide live, synchronous test audio/video data, including disconnecting from the network, blocking or turning off cameras, turning off microphones, closing proctoring pages, or otherwise interfering with normal proctoring;
(10) using unauthorized hardware devices, software, programs, or other technical means;
(11) organizing or participating in cheating, or providing personnel, equipment, accounts, technical support, or other assistance for organized cheating;
(12) other conduct involving cheating or assisting others to cheat by improper means.
5.2.3 The organizer and its authorized providers or staff will deem a test taker to have cheated if any of the following is discovered during or after the test:
(1) you obtain eligibility or scores through forged IDs, certificates, records, or other materials;
(2) answers are determined to be substantially identical during scoring;
(3) test‑room order is disrupted and widespread cheating occurs;
(4) staff members assist with cheating and it is verified after the fact;
(5) system logs, data analysis, audio/video records, or other technical means confirm improper acquisition or attempted acquisition of test items or answers;
(6) other conduct that should be deemed cheating.

(1) disrupt the order of any test center, test room, or scoring site;
(2) refuse or obstruct staff performing their duties;
(3) threaten, insult, defame, falsely accuse, or otherwise infringe the legitimate rights of staff or other test takers;
(4) damage test-room facilities or equipment;
(5) engage in other conduct that disrupts test administration.
5.2.5 Test takers must not engage in the following other misconduct:
(1) forging, altering, buying, selling, renting, lending, or fraudulently using CSCA scores, score reports, Admission Tickets, or related supporting documents;
(2) submitting or using an original score report with an institution or other third party after the test results have been revoked or declared invalid;
(3) using false materials, deception, coercion, bribery, or other improper means to request changes to test data, scores, or misconduct decisions;
(4) concealing, transferring, deleting, or altering evidence, or instructing others to provide false evidence or help cover up or conceal misconduct during misconduct investigations, score verification, or reviews;
