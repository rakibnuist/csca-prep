## 来华留学：选择与未来同行的机遇

**EN title:** Study in China: More Than Just a Degree  
**Published:** 2026-10-12  

中国以不断发展的高等教育体系、日益完善的支持保障和丰富多元的文化体验，吸引着全球学生来华留学。据统计，2024—2025学年在华学习的各类国际学生已超过38万名，来自191个国家和地区。对有意来华攻读本科的国际学生而言，学校与学位认可、留学费用、实践与未来发展、安全与健康、校园生活与文化，是五个尤其值得关注的方面。
[image]

一、院校、专业与学位认可
中国拥有全球规模最大的高等教育体系。在2027年QS世界大学排名中，中国内地（大陆）共有85所高校上榜，数量位居全球第三；其中6所高校进入全球排名前100位，5所高校跻身全球排名前50位。此外，中国多所高校的工程学、计算机科学、材料科学等学科进入基本科学指标数据库（ESI）全球前千分之一。
中国已同183个国家和地区开展教育合作交流，并与61个国家和地区签署学历学位互认协议。截至2024年底，中国800多所高校开设双语课程超过5万门；许多高校还开设全英文授课的本科项目，覆盖商科、工程、医学、计算机等主要学科。
课堂之外，中国高校的科研平台和研究训练项目也为表现优秀的国际学生提供了科研机会；部分项目还提供创新实践或校企合作学习机会。在中国获得的学位，可为国际学生继续深造和职业发展提供有力支撑。
[image]

二、留学费用
合理选择留学院校、城市和生活方式，有助于来华留学生在兼顾学习目标的同时，合理控制留学成本。中国的校园食堂和学生宿舍可提供丰富的日常餐饮与多样化的住宿选择，发达的公共交通网络也有助于控制出行开支，让留学生活更便于规划。
在中国，不同高校、专业和城市的学习与生活成本各异，国际学生可以结合自身预算，做出更适合的选择。在读期间，符合条件的国际学生还可通过申请奖学金或勤工助学岗位，在积累实践经验的同时补贴部分日常开支。
三、实践机会与未来发展
中国拥有完备的工业体系和规模庞大的市场，先进制造、新能源、数字经济等领域的发展，为相关专业的学习与实践提供了丰富场景。中国与世界各地广泛的经贸联系，也为国际学生了解全球产业与市场、接触跨国合作提供了机会。这些产业资源与国际经贸联系，共同为国际学生积累专业实践经验、拓展国际视野和探索多元职业发展路径提供了有利条件。
在中国积累行业实践经验
通过校企合作课程、实习项目和创新创业实践，国际学生有机会接触行业实际需求，加深对专业知识及其应用的理解。参与国际展会、论坛和赛事等活动，也有助于拓宽视野、锻炼沟通与协作能力。
提升面向未来的国际竞争力
在中国求学期间，国际学生不仅能够学习专业知识、提升中文能力，还能通过行业实践和跨文化交流，逐步建立产业联系与人际网络，增进对不同市场和工作方式的理解。这些能力、经验与联系，在与中国有业务往来的工作中尤具价值，也有助于国际学生适应多元文化环境、参与国际团队协作，为继续深造以及在不同国家、行业发展积累优势。
在华求学期间建立的联系，也可以在毕业后延续。来华校友分布在不同国家和行业，通过校友活动保持交流、分享经验和获取职业信息，有助于毕业生拓展视野、发现合作机会，为长远发展提供支持。
[image]

四、安全、健康与支持
在陌生的国家求学，安全的生活环境有助于国际学生安心学习、适应新的生活。根据盖洛普发布的《2025年全球安全报告》，94%的中国受访者表示，在自己居住的城市或地区夜间独自行走时感到安全，这一比例在受调查国家和地区中位居第三。
校园内的安全管理、健康服务和入学支持，也为国际学生提供了日常保障。中国高校通过校园安保、应急联络和新生入学指引等措施，帮助学生熟悉环境、了解求助渠道。按规定购买的医疗保险，可以为国际学生应对疾病和意外带来的医疗支出提供支持。部分高校还可提供就医指引、语言协助或转诊服务，帮助国际学生获得所需的医疗服务。
遇到紧急情况，可拨打110报警电话、119火警电话或120医疗急救电话；出入境政策、住宿登记和证件办理等问题，可通过国家移民管理局12367服务平台咨询。
五、留学生活与文化体验
中国的数字服务为日常生活带来便利。通信套餐选择丰富，学生可根据需求和预算灵活选择；高速移动网络，让学习、交流与日常生活更加顺畅。主流移动支付平台支持绑定符合要求的境外银行卡，方便日常消费；校园卡和线上服务平台，也让借阅、消费等校园事务办理更加便捷。网购、外卖和快递服务，则让日常采购与生活安排更省时省力。
便捷的交通让课堂之外的探索更加丰富。中国拥有全球规模最大的高铁网络，连接众多城市，方便国际学生利用周末或假期走访不同地区。中国已有61项世界遗产、45项非物质文化遗产列入联合国教科文组织名录；从北京中轴线到苏州古典园林，从敦煌莫高窟到红河哈尼梯田，历史遗址与自然景观让书本中的知识变得生动可感。
中国的文化多样性，也体现在56个民族丰富的生活传统中。不同民族的语言、服饰、节庆、艺术和饮食，为国际学生了解中国提供了多元视角。通过旅行、文化体验和与不同民族同学的交流，学生可以进一步了解这些传统在当代生活中的延续与变化。
丰富的饮食文化也是留学生活的一部分。川菜麻辣鲜香，粤菜清鲜精致，江浙菜温润清甜，西北菜粗犷豪放，各地饮食为国际学生带来了多样的味觉体验，也成为了解地方文化的窗口。校园食堂与各地餐饮提供了不同的就餐选择，部分高校食堂还设有清真或素食窗口，方便有不同饮食需求的学生安排日常生活。
在校园中，文化活动为国际学生提供了亲身参与和交流的机会。春节包饺子、元宵做花灯、端午包粽子、中秋做月饼，学生既可以体验中国节庆，也可以向同学介绍家乡的饮食、音乐与传统，在交流中增进理解、建立友谊。书法、国画、茶艺和传统服饰体验等课程与活动，也为感兴趣的学生了解中国文化提供了更多途径。

从专业学习到校园生活，来华留学为国际学生提供了探索兴趣、积累能力与结识朋友的机会。对有意来华攻读本科的学生而言，了解适合自身目标的学校和专业，是规划求学之路的起点。
国际学生可进一步了解目标院校的招生条件与来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（CSCA）成绩要求，并根据所申请专业确定考试科目和考试语言。相关信息可通过本站的考试介绍、考试安排和报名指南查询。
附注：本文数据来源于《学在中国》智库报告（2026年4月）、2027年QS世界大学排名、科睿唯安ESI（2026年9月）、盖洛普《2025年全球安全报告》、联合国教科文组织及中国非物质文化遗产网、国家留学基金管理委员会、《学校招收和培养国际学生管理办法》等公开信息。

### English
China attracts students from around the world with its continued advances in higher education, student support services, and rich cultural experiences. In the 2024–2025 academic year, more than 380,000 international students from 191 countries and regions studied in China.
If you're considering a bachelor's degree in China, here are five key areas deserve your attention: universities and degree recognition, study costs, practical experience and career prospects, safety and health, as well as campus life and culture.
[image]

1. Universities, Programs, and Degree Recognition
With the world's largest higher education system, China offers a wide range of universities degree programs. In the 2027 QS World University Rankings, 85 universities from the Chinese mainland were listed—the third-highest number worldwide. Among them, six ranked among the world's top 100, including five in the top 50. According to Essential Science Indicators (ESI), a number of Chinese universities rank among the world's top 0.1% in research fields such as engineering, computer science, and materials science.
China has established educational cooperation and exchange partnerships with 183 countries and regions and singed degree recognition agreements with 61 countries and regions. By the end of 2024, more than 800 Chinese universities offered over 50,000 bilingual courses. Many also offer English-taught bachelor's programs covering business, engineering, medicine, computer science, and other major disciplines.
Beyond the classroom, some Chinese universities offer research opportunities for high-achieving students. Other programs may also include innovation projects and industry partnerships that provide practical experience. A degree from China could provide a strong foundation for further study and career development.
[image]

2. Study Costs
Choosing universities, cities and lifestyles rationally helps international students properly control their study costs while achieving their academic goals in China. Chinese universities could always offer you a range of options for campus dining and student housing. Moreover, the well-developed public transport network also helps control travel expenses, making overseas study easier to plan.
In China, the costs of study and living vary across different universities, majors and cities. Eligible international students may also apply for scholarships or work-study opportunities to help cover living expenses while gaining practical experience.
3. Practical Experience and Career Prospects
China's extensive industrial base and vast market offer rich opportunities for learning and hands-on experience across industries, from advanced manufacturing and clean energy to the digital economy. Its global trade ties also give students exposure to international markets and cross-border collaboration.
Gain Industry Experience
Internships, industry partnerships, and entrepreneurship programs help students apply their knowledge to real-world challenges. International exhibitions, forums, and competitions also broaden their horizons and strengthen communication and teamwork skills.
Prepare for a Global Career
Studying in China allows students to improve their Chinese, build professional connections, and develop cross-cultural skills. These skills and connections are especially valuable in careers that involve working with China. They also prepare graduates for further study and work in different countries and industries.
Connections made in China can last long after graduation. Alumni networks help graduates stay connected, exchange career advice, and explore opportunities worldwide.
[image]

4. Safety, Health, and Student Support
Studying in a foreign country, a safe living environment helps international students study with peace of mind and adapt to a new life. According to Gallup's 2025 Global Safety Report, 94% of respondents in China felt safe walking alone at night in their local area—the third-highest figure among the countries and regions surveyed.
Many Chinese universities provide campus security, emergency support, and orientation programs to help international students settle in. Comprehensive and effective medical insurance can help cover eligible medical expenses arising from illness or accidents. Some universities also offer medical guidance, language assistance, and referrals to local healthcare providers.
Useful emergency and service numbers:
110 — Police

119 — Fire and rescue

120 — Medical emergencies

12367 — National Immigration Administration hotline for entry and exit policies, accommodation registration, visa and residence permit services

5. Campus Life and Cultural Experiences
Everyday Convenience
Digital services make everyday life more convenient in China. Students can choose from a range of mobile plans, with high-speed networks supporting study and communication. Major mobile payment platforms support eligible international bank cards. Campus cards and online services simplify library access and payments, while online shopping, food delivery, and courier services make everyday errands easier.
Explore China Beyond Campus
China is home to 61 UNESCO World Heritage properties and 45 elements of intangible cultural heritage on UNESCO lists. From Beijing's Central Axis and the Classical Gardens of Suzhou to the Mogao Caves in Dunhuang and the Honghe Hani Rice Terraces, there is much to discover beyond the classroom. With the world's largest high-speed rail network, China makes it easy for students to explore different parts of the country.
China's 56 ethnic groups contribute to its cultural diversity. Their languages, clothing, festivals, arts, and cuisines offer different perspectives on Chinese culture. Travel, cultural activities, and interactions with classmates from different ethnic groups help students discover how these traditions remain part of modern life.
Experience Chinese Food and Traditions
China's regional cuisines offer a wide range of flavors. Sichuan cuisine is known for its bold, spicy dishes; Cantonese cuisine for its fresh, delicate flavors; Jiangsu and Zhejiang cuisines for their mild, slightly sweet flavors; and northwestern China for its hearty specialties. University dining halls and local restaurants offer plenty of choices, including halal and vegetarian options at some campuses.
Cultural activities bring you and your fellow students together throughout the year. You might make dumplings during Spring Festival, craft lanterns for the Lantern Festival, prepare zongzi (sticky rice dumplings) for the Dragon Boat Festival, or make mooncakes during the Mid-Autumn Festival. You can also share your own food, music, and traditions through these cultural exchanges. Workshops in Chinese calligraphy, traditional painting, tea culture, and traditional clothing at many universities offer further ways to experience Chinese culture firsthand.
Start Your Journey to China
From academic study and practical training to cultural experiences and new friendships, studying in China offers many ways to explore your interests and build skills. Start by finding universities and programs that match your interests and goals.
Before applying, check your chosen university's admission requirements, including any China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) score requirements. Confirm the test subjects and test language required for your intended program. Visit the Exam Overview, Exam Schedule, and Registration Guide sections of this website for details.

## 2026年11月–2027年6月CSCA考试安排

**EN title:** CSCA Test Schedule for November 2026 – June 2027  
**Published:** 2026-09-24  

为帮助各位考生做好报考和备考规划，现公布2026年11月至2027年6月CSCA考试安排及有关调整事项。
一、考试计划
每场考试分两个考试日开展：首日设置专业中文、数学科目；次日设置物理、化学科目。受场次安排影响，首科开考时间为北京时间08:00或14:00。请考生务必仔细查阅下表，确认意向报考场次下各科目的准确开考时间。
2026年11月场次报名通道将于10月中旬开启，请考生持续关注CSCA官方网站的最新通知。

[image]

居家网考与线下集中机考同步举行，两类考试的试题内容、考试时长及评分标准一致。身处其他时区的考生，请将上述北京时间（UTC+8）自行换算为当地时间。
若最终考试安排发生调整，请以当次考试通知及准考证信息为准。
二、考试成绩发布时间
自2026年11月场次考试起，考试成绩将于考试结束后10个工作日内予以公布。请考生结合成绩发布时间，合理规划院校申请与材料提交。

For sessions from November 2026 onwards, examination results will be released within 10 working days after the exam. Candidates are advised to plan their university applications and document submissions accordingly.
