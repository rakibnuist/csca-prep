# 常见问题

报名与资格
1. 哪些人可以报考CSCA？
答：CSCA专为计划申请中国大陆高校本科课程的非中国籍学生设计，持中国护照或港澳台居民居住证者不可报考。
2. 报名是否有年龄限制？
答：没有年龄限制，但18周岁以下考生必须由监护人同意并协助完成报名。
3. 如何报名？
答：考生需在CSCA官网注册账号、填写信息并完成缴费，收到确认邮件即报名成功。
4. 报名时需要准备什么？
答：建议考生在报名前准备好以下材料和信息：
1. 长期有效的电子邮箱（用于接收确认邮件和通知）；
2. 有效身份证件（具体要求详见）；
3. 电子证件照（具体要求详见）；
4. 银行卡信息（具体要求详见）
5. 报名必须要用护照吗？如果我的护照即将过期怎么办？
答：护照是唯一可接受的身份证件。若您的护照即将过期，请尽快办理更新手续。您必须确保在考试当天出示的护照为原件且在有效期内，过期或复印件均不被接受，否则将无法参加考试。
6. 我该如何选择报考的科目？
答：您的科目选择应主要取决于您计划申请的大学和专业的要求。我们建议您提前查阅目标院校的招生简章，了解其对CSCA考试科目的具体建议或要求。
7. 每次考试最多可以报考几门科目？
答：目前没有报考数量限制，建议考生根据自身备考情况和目标院校要求合理选择科目。
8. 不同的科目可以选择不同的考试语言吗？
答：可以。对于基础科目（数学、物理、化学），您可以为每个科目单独选择用中文或英文进行考试。 而专业中文的所有科目仅提供中文考试。
最终考试语言以申请院校的要求为准；如院校无明确要求，考生可自行选择。
9. 提交报名后，我还能修改报名信息吗？
答：报名信息一经提交即被锁定，考生不能在系统中自行修改。如需更正，考生必须在准考证生成前提交信息更正申请。准考证生成后，所有信息均不得修改。
10. 能否转考或退考？
答：CSCA仅支持退考，不可转考。考生须在准考证发放前提交退考申请；准考证生成后不再接受退考。
11. 我可以多次参加CSCA考试吗？有次数限制吗？
答：可以。CSCA不限制考试次数，考生可多次报考。成绩有效期为两年。在申请时，考生通常可以提交有效期内的最佳成绩，但具体要求以目标院校政策为准。
12. 准考证什么时候可以下载？
答：准考证将于考试前在报名系统开放下载，考生可登录系统自行打印。系统开放下载时，会同步向考生注册邮箱发送通知邮件。
13. 准考证信息有误怎么办？
答：准考证信息生成后不能修改。信息与证件不符将导致无法入场。请务必在报名时仔细核对，并在下载准考证后再次确认。
考前准备
14. 参加居家网考需要准备哪些设备？
答：考生需准备一台符合要求的电脑（主机位）和一部手机/平板（副机位）。居家网考要求双机位监控，主机位用于答题及正面监控，副机位从考生侧后方 45°角拍摄全程。 具体设备要求和副机位摆放细节请参见“设备与网络要求”。
15. 网络速度和稳定性有什么要求？
答：居家网考要求上下行对称带宽不低于 20Mbps，系统连接网速不少于 500KB/s，建议使用有线宽带直连，并在考试当天关闭所有无关软件和后台程序。 考生可在客户端内使用测速功能验证连接质量，同时建议准备备用 4G 热点，以便网络异常时快速切换。
16. 考试环境需要怎样布置？
答：考生必须在独立、安静、光线充足的封闭空间完成考试，桌面保持整洁。禁止在床、沙发等非标准位置答题。具体布置要求详见“考试环境布置”。
17. 居家网考允许使用草稿纸吗？
答： 不允许使用传统的纸质草稿纸、便签等纸张类物品。
但是，您可以使用以下指定的草稿工具：
• 小型白板，配合可擦写记号笔。
• 或将一张纸置于透明文件袋中，同样使用可擦写记号笔在文件袋表面书写。
18. 考试前需要安装什么软件？
答：主机位电脑需提前安装 CSCA 指定考试客户端，副机位手机/平板需安装监控 App。 具体安装指南及下载链接将通过 CSCA 官方报名系统和考生注册邮箱同步发布，请务必注意查收并提前完成安装。
19. 在安装软件过程中遇到问题怎么办？
答：若遇到安装问题，建议您：
1. 优先自查：请首先查阅官方提供的操作指南与常见问题解答，按步骤排查。
2. 寻求支持：若问题仍在，您可以在平台测试开放期间进入技术支持会议室，获得一对一协助；或通过官方客服提交问题截图，我们将尽快为您解答。
20. 正式考试前是否需要参加考试系统个人测试和集中模拟测试？
答：必须参加。所有考生均须按要求完成以下两项考前测试：
• 考试系统个人测试：用于验证您的设备、网络及系统环境是否符合考试要求。
• 集中模拟测试：将完整模拟真实考试的全流程，是您提前熟悉操作、排查潜在问题的深度演练。
⚠ 考生因未按要求完成上述测试，导致正式考试当天无法正常参加或完成考试的，相关责任由考生本人承担，考试费用不予退还。
考试当天流程与规则
21. 我需要提前多久进入考试系统？迟到还能参加考试吗？
答：考生可在考试开始前60分钟起登录考试系统，完成设备检测、人脸识别与身份验证。考试正式开始后将禁止进入考试系统。
22. 考试当天需要携带哪些物品？
答：考试当天，考生须准备以下物品：1. 有效身份证件原件；2. 纸质准考证；3. 小型白板/置于透明文件袋中的纸张与可擦写记号笔。 具体要求请参见“考试环境布置”。
23. 考试当天忘带准考证或身份证件怎么办？
答：准考证包含考试登录所需的账号和密码，身份证件则用于身份核验。若缺少纸质准考证，您将无法登录系统参加考试；若缺少有效身份证件，则无法通过身份核验进入考试。无论缺少哪一项，均视为缺考，考试费用不予退还。请务必提前准备，避免因遗漏错过考试。
24. 我能用电子版身份证件进行身份核验吗？
答：不能。居家网考身份核验必须使用有效身份证件原件，电子版或复印件均不被接受。
25. 如果在考试中遇到技术问题该怎么办？
您可以通过考试客户端或者监考App呼叫监考老师寻求帮助。技术支持团队和监考员会提供远程协助。
26. 考试过程中可以提前交卷吗？
答：不可以。所有考生须在规定考试时间内完成作答，考试结束后系统会统一收卷。 除突发健康或紧急情况经主考批准外，提前交卷或擅自离场均视为违规。
考试成绩
27. 成绩什么时候公布？
答：考试结束后10个工作日内公布。如有特殊情况导致延迟，将在官网发布公告。
28. 如何获取和寄送成绩单？
答：成绩公布后，考生可登录CSCA官方报名网站下载电子版成绩报告。该电子报告为官方正式文件，所有认可CSCA成绩的院校均接受此版本，无需额外申请寄送，考生可直接提交给申请院校。
29. 中国高校如何使用 CSCA 成绩？
答：认可CSCA成绩的高校在招生审核时，可通过官方系统核验成绩单真伪，并同步查看该成绩在全球考生中的百分位排名。这一排名有助招生官直观了解申请者的全球学术竞争力，并结合本校标准进行综合评估。

## English
A. Registration & Eligibility
1. Who is eligible to take the CSCA?
The CSCA is designed specifically for non-Chinese citizens planning to apply for undergraduate programs at universities in Mainland China.
Individuals holding Chinese passports or residence permits for Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan are not eligible to register.
2. Is there an age limit for registration?
There is no age limit. However, candidates under the age of 18 must have parental consent and assistance to complete registration.
3. How do I register?
Candidates must create an account on the official CSCA website, complete all required information, and pay the test fee.
Registration is considered complete once the candidate receives a confirmation email.
4. What should I prepare before registration?
We recommend preparing the following materials and information in advance:
• A valid and long-term accessible email address (for receiving confirmations and notifications)
• A valid identification document; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
• A digital ID photo; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
• Bank card information; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
5. Is a passport mandatory for registration? What if my passport is about to expire?
The passport is the only acceptable form of identification. If your passport is about to expire, please renew it as soon as possible. You must present an original, valid passport on the test day. Expired or photocopied passports will not be accepted, and you will not be allowed to take the exam.
6. How should I choose my test subjects?
Your subject choices should depend primarily on the requirements of your intended universities and programs.
We recommend reviewing the admission guidelines of your target institutions to understand their specific subject recommendations or CSCA requirements.
7. What is the maximum number of subjects I can take per test session?
There is currently no limit on the number of subjects per session.
Candidates are advised to select subjects reasonably, based on their preparation and target university requirements.
8. Can I choose different test languages for different subjects?
Yes. For foundational subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry), you may choose either Chinese or English for each subject.
Professional Chinese subjects are offered in Chinese only.
Candidates shall take the test in the language required by their target institution. Where no requirement is specified, they may choose at their discretion.
9. Can I modify my registration information after submission?
Once submitted, registration information is locked and cannot be modified by the candidate. If corrections are necessary, a modification request must be submitted before the Admission Ticket is generated.
No changes can be made once the ticket has been issued.
10. Can I transfer my registration or withdraw from the test?
Only withdrawals are accepted for CSCA; test transfers are not permitted.Candidates must submit a withdrawal request before the Admission Ticket is generated. Requests made after the ticket has been generated will not be accepted.
11. Can I take the CSCA multiple times? Is there a limit?
Yes. The CSCA places no restriction on the number of attempts.
Each score is valid for two years.
Candidates may submit their best valid score when applying, subject to the policies of the receiving institution.
12. When can I download my Admission Ticket?
Admission Tickets will be available for download in the registration system before the test.
Candidates will also receive an email notification when the download option becomes available.
13. What should I do if there is an error on my Admission Ticket?
Admission Ticket information cannot be modified after generation.
Any discrepancy between your ID and ticket details will result in denied entry.
Please double-check all information during registration and verify again after downloading your ticket.
B. Pre-Test Preparation
14. What equipment do I need for the RHT?
Candidates must prepare a compliant primary device (computer) and a secondary device (smartphone or tablet).
The RHT requires dual-device monitoring: the primary device is used for answering questions and and front-facing monitoring, while the secondary device - positioned at a 45°rear-side angle - captures the entire test session.
Please refer to the Device and Network Requirements section for detailed specifications.
15. What are the network speed and stability requirements?
The RHT requires a symmetrical upload/download bandwidth of at least 20 Mbps, and a connection speed to the test system of at least 500 KB/s.
A wired broadband connection is strongly recommended.
Close all non-essential software and background processes on Test Day.
Candidates can use the built-in speed test feature in the Test Client to verify connection quality, and should prepare a backup 4G mobile hotspot in case of network disruption.
16. How should I set up my test environment?
Candidates must take the RHT in an independent, quiet, and well-lit enclosed room, using a clean and organized desk.
Taking the test on a bed, sofa, or any non-standard surface is prohibited.
See Test Environment Setup section for detailed requirements.
17. Are scratch papers allowed for the RHT?
Traditional paper scratch sheets, sticky notes, or any other paper-based materials are not allowed.
However, candidates may use one of the following approved scratch tools:
• A small whiteboard with an erasable marker; or
• A blank sheet of paper placed inside a transparent file bag, using an erasable marker on the bag surface.
18. What software must I install before the test?
The primary device (computer) must have the official CSCA Test Client installed.
The secondary device (phone or tablet) must have the designated monitoring app installed.
Installation guides and download links will be provided via the CSCA registration system and the candidate's registered email.
Please complete installation in advance.
19. What should I do if I encounter issues during software installation?
If you experience installation problems:
• Self-check: Review the official operation guide and troubleshooting instructions.
• Seek support: If the issue persists, you may join a technical support session during the platform testing period for one-on-one assistance, or submit screenshots of the issue via official support channels.
20. Do I need to complete the Equipment and Software Check and the Proctored Mock Test before the official test?
Yes. All test takers must complete the following pre-test checks as required:
• Equipment and Software Check: Checks whether your device, network, and system environment meet the test requirements.
• Home-based Proctored Mock Tests: Simulates the complete official test process and helps you become familiar with the test procedures and identify potential issues before test day.
⚠ Failure to complete the required pre-test checks may result in being unable to participate in or complete the official test on test day. Any resulting consequences shall be borne by the test taker, and the test fee will not be refunded.
C. Test-Day Procedures & Rules
21. How early should I log in to the system? Can I still take the test if I'm late?
Candidates may log in up to 60 minutes before the test begins to complete device checks, facial recognition, and identity verification.
Entry is not permitted after the test officially starts.
22. What items do I need to prepare on Test Day?
Candidates must have the following items ready:
• A valid original ID document
• A printed Admission Ticket
• A small whiteboard or a blank sheet inside a transparent file bag, and an erasable marker
Refer to Test Environment Setup for detailed requirements.
23. What if I forget my Admission Ticket or ID document?
The Admission Ticket contains login credentials, and the ID document is required for verification.
Without either, you cannot access the test system.
In both cases, the candidate will be marked as absent, and no refund will be issued.
Please prepare these items in advance to avoid missing the test.
24. Can I use a digital copy of my ID for verification?
Candidates can contact the proctor through the Test Client or monitoring app for assistance.
The technical support team and proctors will provide remote troubleshooting help.
25. What should I do if I encounter technical problems during the test?
Candidates can contact the proctor through the Test Client or monitoring app for assistance.
The technical support team and proctors will provide remote troubleshooting help.
26. Can I submit my test early?
No. All candidates must complete the test within the allotted time.
The system will automatically submit responses when time expires.
Early submission or leaving without authorization will be considered a violation, except in approved medical or emergency cases.
D. Test Scores
27. When will the test results be released?
Results will be released within ten (10) working days after the test.
If there are delays due to special circumstances, a public notice will be posted on the official website.
28. How can I obtain and send my score report?
Once results are released, candidates can log into the official CSCA registration system to download the electronic score report.
This e-report is an official and valid document recognized by all institutions accepting CSCA results.
No additional score-sending request is required; candidates can submit it directly to their application institutions.
29. How do Chinese universities use CSCA scores?
Universities recognizing CSCA results can verify the authenticity of score reports via the official system and view the candidate's global percentile ranking.
This ranking helps admissions officers assess applicants academic competitiveness globally and make comprehensive evaluations based on institutional standards.
