# 成绩与报告

1. 成绩公布
考试成绩通常在考试结束后10个工作日内公布。请登录CSCA官方报名网站查询并下载电子版成绩报告。 如有特殊情况导致延迟，将在官网发布公告。
2. 成绩效力
• 用途：该成绩是申请中国高校本科录取的重要参考依据，同时也是中国政府奖学金评审的必备申请材料。
• 有效期：自证书发布之日起2年内有效。

## English
1. Score Release
Exam scores are typically released within 10 business days after your test date. Log in to your CSCA account at www.csca.cn to view and download the electronic score report. Notice will be posted on the official website if delays occur.
2. Score Validity & Use
* Purpose: The CSCA score serves as an important reference for undergraduate admissions to Chinese universities and is required for the evaluation of Chinese Government Scholarships.
* Validity Period: The score report is valid for two (2) years from the date of issuance.
