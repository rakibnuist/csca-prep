# 考试规则

1. 入场与证件
• 考生须按准考证上标注的时间，提前至少40分钟抵达指定考场。
• 必须携带：有效身份证件原件 + 纸质准考证。
• 证件与准考证信息须完全一致；证件过期、信息不符、或人证不一致者将被拒绝入场。
• 考生入场时须通过人脸识别或人工审核。
• 开考后（考试正式开始时间起），一律不得入场，按缺考处理。
2. 随身物品与违禁物
• 严禁携带：手机、智能手表/手环、蓝牙耳机、录音/摄像设备、书籍资料、纸条、计算器、包袋、食品饮料等。
• 允许携带：身份证件、准考证、黑色签字笔。
• 特殊情况：经考点同意，可携带无标签饮用水，放置于地面。
3. 考场纪律
• 证件展示：考生须将身份证件和准考证放置在桌面左上角，便于监考人员随时查验。
• 物品存放：除规定的考试文具和去包装饮用水外，所有其他个人物品（含手机等电子设备，须确认关机）必须存至指定区域，不得带至座位。
• 草稿纸：草稿纸由考点统一发放，考试结束后须全部收回，不得带离考场。
• 禁止交流：考试全程须保持安静，独立完成作答，严禁交头接耳、传递物品或进行任何形式的交流。如有问题，请举手示意，由监考员前来处理。
• 严禁作弊：考试期间禁止使用手机、智能手表等任何通讯或摄录设备，禁止查阅资料或抄袭他人答案。任何形式的作弊行为都将被记录并严肃处理。
• 离场规则：考试期间未经监考人员允许，不得以任何理由离开座位或提前退场。如需去洗手间，必须由工作人员陪同。
4. 违规处理
违规行为一经查实，将根据情节严重程度给予以下处理：
• 情节较轻：取消当次考试成绩。
• 情节较重：取消当次考试成绩，且自违规之日起两年内不得再次报考。
• 情节严重：取消所有历史成绩，并终身禁止参加 CSCA 考试。
• 情节特别严重：可能将移交至司法机关依法处理，并影响在华留学资格及签证申请。

## English
1. Entry & Identification Documents
* Arrive at the designated test center at least 40 minutes before the time shown on your Admission Ticket.
* Bring Both: (1) an original, valid, government-issued, photo ID, and (2) printed CSCA Admission Ticket.
* The ID information must exactly match the Admission Ticket. Expired IDs, data mismatches, or a mismatch between the candidate's appearance and the ID photo will result in denied entry.
* Facial verification or manual ID verification will be conducted at check-in.
* No late entry: under no circumstances will candidates be admitted to the test room after the the official start time. Late arrivals are recorded as absent.
2. Personal Belongings & Prohibited Items
* Strictly Prohibited: Mobile phones, smartwatches/wristbands, Bluetooth earphones, recording/photography devices, books/reference materials, notes, calculators, bags, food, drinks, etc.
* Permitted: ID document, CSCA Admission Ticket, black ink pens (gel, non-erasable).
* Exception: With approval of test center, plain water in a clear, label-free bottle may be brought in and must be placed on the floor.
3. Test Center Discipline
* ID on Desk: Place your ID document and Admission Ticket at the top-left of the desk for inspection at any time.
* Item Storage: All personal items not permitted (including powered-off electronic devices) must be stored in the designated area and are not allowed to be kept at the test desk.
* Scratch Paper: Provided by the test center and must be returned in full(used and unused) at the end of the exam; removal from the test room is prohibited.
* No Communication: Complete silence must be maintained throughout the exam. Work independently. Whispering, passing items, or any form of communication is strictly prohibited. Raise your hand if you have a question, and an invigilator will assist you.
* No Cheating: Using mobile phones, smartwatches, or any communication or recording devices, consulting materials, or copying answers is prohibited during the exam. Any form of cheating will be recorded and dealt with seriously.
* Leaving the Room: Do not leave your seats or the exam room early without proctor permission. Restroom visit, if necessary, must be accompanied by the designated staff.
4. Consequences for Violations
Verified violations may result in the following actions based on severity:
* Minor: Invalidation of the current exam scores.
* Serious: Invalidation of the current exam scores and a two-year registration ban from the decision date.
* Severe: Invalidation of all previously awarded CSCA scores and permanent disqualification from future CSCA exams.
* Extremely Severe: Referral to to law-enforcement/judicial authorities in accordance with applicable laws; may affect eligibility to study in China and future visa applications.
