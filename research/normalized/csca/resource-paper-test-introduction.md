# 考试简介

为确保考试组织的统一性与安全性，2025年12月21日举行的全球统一考试暂不启用纸笔考试形式。
如后续恢复纸笔考试，请关注CSCA考试官方网站的最新公告。

## English
To ensure the consistency and security of examination administration, the paper-based test will not be offered for the global unified CSCA examination on December 21, 2025. Please refer to the official CSCA website for any updates regarding the reinstatement of this format.
