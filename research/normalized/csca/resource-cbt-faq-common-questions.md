# 常见问题

报名与资格
1. 哪些人可以报考CSCA？
答：CSCA专为计划申请中国大陆高校本科课程的非中国籍学生设计，持中国护照或港澳台居民居住证者不可报考。
2. 报名是否有年龄限制？
答：没有年龄限制，但18周岁以下考生必须由监护人同意并协助完成报名。
3. 如何报名？
答：考生需在CSCA官网注册账号、填写信息并完成缴费，收到确认邮件即报名成功。
4. 报名时需要准备什么？
答：建议考生在报名前准备好以下材料和信息：
1. 长期有效的电子邮箱（用于接收确认邮件和通知）；
2. 有效身份证件（具体要求详见）；
3. 电子证件照（具体要求详见）；
4. 银行卡信息（具体要求详见）
5. 报名必须要用护照吗？如果我的护照即将过期怎么办？
答：护照是唯一可接受的身份证件。若您的护照即将过期，请尽快办理更新手续。您必须确保在考试当天出示的护照为原件且在有效期内，过期或复印件均不被接受，否则将无法参加考试。
6. 我该如何选择报考的科目？
答：您的科目选择应主要取决于您计划申请的大学和专业的要求。我们建议您提前查阅目标院校的招生简章，了解其对CSCA考试科目的具体建议或要求。
7. 每次考试最多可以报考几门科目？
答：目前没有报考数量限制，建议考生根据自身备考情况和目标院校要求合理选择科目。
8. 不同的科目可以选择不同的考试语言吗？
答：可以。对于基础科目（数学、物理、化学），您可以为每个科目单独选择用中文或英文进行考试。 而专业中文的所有科目仅提供中文考试。
9. 提交报名后，我还能修改报名信息吗？
答：报名信息一经提交即被锁定，考生不能在系统中自行修改。如需更正，考生必须在准考证生成前提交信息更正申请。准考证生成后，所有信息均不得修改。
10. 如何申请特殊考试安排？
答：考生需在考试日前 30 天提交正式申请，并提供相应的证明材料。具体流程请参见“特殊考试安排”。 如因条件限制无法提供便利，可选择取消报名并获得全额退费。
11. 能否转考或退考？
答：CSCA仅支持退考，不可转考。考生须在准考证发放前提交退考申请；准考证生成后不再接受退考。
12. 我可以多次参加CSCA考试吗？有次数限制吗？
答：可以。CSCA不限制考试次数，考生可多次报考。成绩有效期为两年。在申请时，考生通常可以提交有效期内的最佳成绩，但具体要求以目标院校政策为准。
13. 准考证什么时候可以下载？
答：准考证将于考试前在报名系统开放下载，考生可登录系统自行打印。系统开放下载时，会同步向考生注册邮箱发送通知邮件。
14. 准考证信息有误怎么办？
答：准考证信息生成后不能修改。信息与证件不符将导致无法入场。请务必在报名时仔细核对，并在下载准考证后再次确认。
考试当天流程与规则
15. 我需要提前多久到达考场？迟到还能进场考试吗？
答：考生须按准考证标注时间提前至少40分钟抵达考场。考试正式开始后不得入场。
16. 考试当天需要携带哪些物品？
答：考试当天，考生须携带以下物品入场：1. 纸质准考证；2.有效身份证件原件；3.黑色签字笔。具体要求请参见“考试规则”。
17. 考试当天忘带准考证或身份证件怎么办？
答：准考证是进入考场的必备凭证。缺少准考证或有效身份证件任一项，考生均不得入场，并视为缺考，考试费用不予退还。为确保您顺利应试，请您务必谨慎对待，避免因证件遗漏错过考试机会。
18. 我能用电子版准考证（手机出示）入场吗？
答：不能。考生必须携带纸质准考证，并与有效身份证件原件一同出示，方可入场。
19. 考试中可以做笔记吗？
答：可以。考点将统一发放草稿纸，考试结束后须全部收回。
20. 考试中可以上洗手间吗？
答：如确需离场，必须在监考人员陪同下进行。离场时间过长或产生违规行为，将按违纪处理。
21. 如果电脑、网络或系统出现故障怎么办？会补时吗？
答：出现故障时，请立即举手求助监考老师。如因考点设备或系统故障导致中断，监考老师会协助重启或更换设备，系统会保留已完成的作答进度，并可酌情补时；如因考生个人误操作导致，则不予补时。
22. 考试过程中可以提前交卷吗？
答：不可以。所有考生须在规定考试时间内完成作答，考试结束后系统会统一收卷。除突发健康或紧急情况经主考批准外，提前交卷或擅自离场均视为违规。
考试成绩
23. 成绩什么时候公布？
答：考试结束后10个工作日内公布。如有特殊情况导致延迟，将在官网发布公告。
24. 如何获取和寄送成绩单？
答：成绩公布后，考生可登录CSCA官方报名网站下载电子版成绩报告。该电子报告为官方正式文件，所有认可CSCA成绩的院校均接受此版本，无需额外申请寄送，考生可直接提交给申请院校。
25. 中国高校如何使用 CSCA 成绩？
答：认可CSCA成绩的高校在招生审核时，可通过官方系统核验成绩单真伪，并同步查看该成绩在全球考生中的百分位排名。 这一排名有助招生官直观了解申请者的全球学术竞争力，并结合本校标准进行综合评估。

## English
A. Registration & Eligibility
1. Who is eligible to take the CSCA?
The CSCA is designed specifically for non-Chinese citizens planning to apply for undergraduate programs at universities in Mainland China. Individuals holding Chinese passports or residence permits for Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan are not eligible to register.
2. Is there an age limit for registration?
There is no age limit. However, candidates under the age of 18 must have parental consent and assistance to complete registration.
3. How do I register?
Candidates must create an account on the official CSCA website, complete all required information, and pay the test fee. Registration is considered complete once the candidate receives a confirmation email.
4. What should I prepare before registration?
We recommend preparing the following materials and information in advance:
• A valid and long-term accessible email address (for receiving confirmations and notifications)
• A valid identification document; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
• A digital ID photo; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
• Bank card information; for specific requirements, please visit www.csca.cn.
5. Is a passport mandatory for registration? What if my passport is about to expire?
The passport is the only acceptable form of identification. If your passport is about to expire, please renew it as soon as possible. You must present an original, valid passport on the test day. Expired or photocopied passports will not be accepted, and you will not be allowed to take the exam.
6. How should I choose my test subjects?
Your subject choices should depend primarily on the requirements of your intended universities and programs. We recommend reviewing the admission guidelines of your target institutions to understand their specific subject recommendations or CSCA requirements.
7. What is the maximum number of subjects I can take per exam session?
There is currently no limit on the number of subjects per session. Candidates are advised to select subjects reasonably, based on their preparation and target university requirements.
8. Can I choose different exam languages for different subjects?
Yes. For foundational subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry), you may choose either Chinese or English for each subject. Professional Chinese subjects are offered in Chinese only.
Candidates shall take the test in the language required by their target institution. Where no requirement is specified, they may choose at their discretion.
9. Can I modify my registration information after submission?
Once submitted, registration information is locked and cannot be modified by the candidate. If corrections are necessary, a modification request must be submitted before the Admission Ticket is generated.
No changes can be made once the ticket has been issued.
10. How do I apply for special testing arrangements?
Candidates must submit a formal application no less than 30 days prior to the exam date and provide relevant supporting documents. Specific procedures can be found in the “Special Accommodations”. If accommodations cannot be provided due to limitations, candidates may choose to cancel their registration and receive a full refund.
11. Can I transfer my registration or withdraw from the test?
Only withdrawals are accepted for CSCA; test transfers are not permitted. Candidates must submit a withdrawal request before the Admission Ticket is generated. Requests made after the ticket has been generated will not be accepted.
12. Can I take the CSCA exam multiple times? Are there limits?
Yes. The CSCA places no restriction on the number of attempts. Each score is valid for two years. Candidates may submit their best valid score when applying, subject to the policies of the receiving institution.
13. When can I download my test admission ticket?
Admission Tickets will be available for download in the registration system before the test. Candidates will also receive an email notification when the download option becomes available.
14. What should I do if there is an error on my Admission Ticket?
Admission Ticket information cannot be modified after generation. Any discrepancy between your ID and ticket details will result in denied entry. Please double-check all information during registration and verify again after downloading your ticket.
B. Exam Day Process & Rules
15. How early should I arrive at the test center? Can I enter if I'm late?
Candidates must arrive at least 40 minutes early. Entry is not allowed after the exam officially starts.
16. What items do I need to bring on exam day?
On test day, candidates must bring: 1. CSCA Admission Ticket; 2. a Valid original ID Document; 3. a Black Ink Pen. See “Exam Rules” for details.
17. What if I forget my test admission ticket or ID on exam day?
The admission ticket is mandatory for entry. Missing either the paper admission ticket or the original valid ID will result in denial of entry, being marked absent, and forfeiture of the exam fee. Please be diligent to avoid missing the exam due to forgotten documents.
18. Can I use a digital copy of my ID for verification?
No. Candidates must bring a printed CSCA admission ticket and present it alongside the original valid ID for entry.
19. Can I take notes during the exam?
Yes. The test center will provide scratch paper, which must be returned entirely after the exam.
20. Can I use the restroom during the exam?
If necessary, you must be accompanied by test center staff. Prolonged absence or any misconduct during the break may be treated as a violation.
21. What if the computer, network, or system fails? Will I get extra time?
Immediately raise your hand to alert a proctor. If the interruption is attributable to test-center equipment, network, or system failure, the proctor will assist with restarting the workstation or relocating you to another seat. Your progress is saved automatically, and compensatory time may be granted to reflect the verified interruption (based on system logs and proctor verification). If the issue results from candidate error (e.g., unplugging cables, closing the test window, ignoring proctor instructions), no additional time will be provided. Do not troubleshoot on your own, restart the computer, or leave your seat without permission.
22. Can I submit my test early during the exam?
No. All candidates must complete the exam within the allotted time; the system will collectively submit answers at the end. Leaving early or exiting without permission is considered a violation, except in cases of sudden health or emergency situations approved by the chief examiner.
C. Exam Scores
23. When will the test results be released?
Results will be released within ten (10) working days after the test. If there are delays due to special circumstances, a public notice will be posted on the official website.
24. How can I obtain and send my score report?
Once results are released, candidates can log into the official CSCA registration system to download the electronic score report.
This e-report is an official and valid document recognized by all institutions accepting CSCA results. No additional score-sending request is required; candidates can submit it directly to their application institutions.
25. How do Chinese universities use CSCA scores?
Universities recognizing CSCA results can verify the authenticity of score reports via the official system and view the candidate's global percentile ranking. This ranking helps admissions officers assess applicants academic competitiveness globally and make comprehensive evaluations based on institutional standards.
