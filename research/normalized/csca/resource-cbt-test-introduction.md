# 考试简介

CSCA集中机考是在授权考点内统一组织的计算机化考试。考点提供规范的考试环境、全程监控及技术支持，确保考试安全公平。
考试在内容、时长及评分标准上与其他考试形式保持一致。考生须凭有效身份证件原件及准考证参加考试。

## English
CSCA Computer-Based Testing (CBT) is an on-site exam administered under uniform procedures at authorized test centers. These centers provide standardized testing environments, continuous proctoring, and technical support to ensure the security, integrity, and fairness of the exam.
The exam's content, duration, and scoring criteria are identical to those of other delivery modes. Candidates are required to present a valid ID document and the printed CSCA Admission Ticket to be admitted to the test room.
