## 2026年11月–2027年6月CSCA考试安排

**EN title:** CSCA Test Schedule for November 2026 – June 2027  
**Published:** 2026-07-24  

为帮助各位考生做好报考和备考规划，现公布2026年11月至2027年6月CSCA考试安排及有关调整事项。
一、考试计划
每场考试分两个考试日开展：首日设置专业中文、数学科目；次日设置物理、化学科目。受场次安排影响，首科开考时间为北京时间08:00或14:00。请考生务必仔细查阅下表，确认意向报考场次下各科目的准确开考时间。
2026年11月场次报名通道将于10月中旬开启，请考生持续关注CSCA官方网站的最新通知。
[image]

居家网考与线下集中机考同步举行，两类考试的试题内容、考试时长及评分标准一致。身处其他时区的考生，请将上述北京时间（UTC+8）自行换算为当地时间。
若最终考试安排发生调整，请以当次考试通知及准考证信息为准。
二、考试成绩发布时间
自2026年11月场次考试起，考试成绩将于考试结束后10个工作日内予以公布。请考生结合成绩发布时间，合理规划院校申请与材料提交。

### English
To help candidates make well‑informed plans for registration and exam preparation, we are pleased to release the CSCA examination timetable from November 2026 to June 2027, along with associated updates.
1. Test Schedule
Each test session spans two days. Professional Chinese and Mathematics are scheduled on Day 1, while Physics and Chemistry are held on Day 2. Depending on the session, the first subject will commence at either 08:00 or 14:00 Beijing Time (UTC+8). Candidates are strongly advised to check the table below for the exact start‑time of each subject for their intended session.
Registration for the November 2026 session will open in mid‑October. Please keep track of updates on the official CSCA website.

[image]

Home-based online exams and computer-based exams at physical test centers will run simultaneously with the same content, duration and scoring standards. Candidates in other time zones shall convert the above‑stated Beijing Time (UTC+8) to their local time.
Any changes to examination arrangements are subject to the respective test notice and admission ticket.
2. Release of Examination Results
For sessions from November 2026 onwards, examination results will be released within 10 working days after the exam. Candidates are advised to plan their university applications and document submissions accordingly.
