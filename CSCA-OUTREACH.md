# CSCA outreach — ready-to-send kit

Two campaigns. Both use the same asset: the free paper library, which costs the recipient nothing and
is genuinely useful to their applicants.

**Rules of engagement**

- One email, one ask, no attachment, no tracking pixel. Follow up **once** after 8 business days.
- Never claim affiliation with CSC, never claim a university "requires" something we cannot source.
- If someone says no, record it and move on; a second follow-up is spam.

## Campaign C — media and press

**Story:** *"A prep site rebuilt the official CSCA test client — because nobody else had."*

Facts to offer a journalist: the five papers and their lengths; the documented client sequence
(system check → Integrity Commitment → full-screen start → left palette with green answered numbers →
no early submission); 2,867 questions; every fact dated and sourced; independent, not affiliated.

Targets: study-abroad media in Bangladesh, Nigeria, Vietnam and Indonesia; Chinese education
trade press; scholarship YouTube channels (offer the chaptered scripts as a collaboration).

**Pitch (120 words max)**

> Subject: The CSCA has a test client nobody was preparing candidates for
>
> Hello — most CSCA prep is PDFs and videos. The actual exam is a Windows client with a system check,
> an Integrity Commitment form, full-screen lock, a left-hand palette that turns green as you answer,
> and no early submission. Candidates lose sittings to that, not to the maths.
>
> We rebuilt the sequence as a browser simulation, using CSCA's own Device & Environment document, and
> published a free paper library in the official format (176 papers, 48/60 and 80/90). Everything is
> dated and sourced to csca.cn, and we are not affiliated with CSC.
>
> Happy to send the screenshots and the sourcing notes if it is useful for a piece.
> [Name] · cscamaster.com
